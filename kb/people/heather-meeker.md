# Heather Meeker

General Partner, OSS Capital

- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

As an internationally known expert in software licensing for over 20 years, Heather is a thought leader and trusted advisor on the use of open source software in business and has helped scores of emerging companies structure licensing and business models.

As a lawyer in private practice, Heather has been recognized with many awards for her work in law and technology, and has advised clients from startups to global enterprises on software licenses and software copyright. She is a prolific writer and speaker on software licensing, and is the author of Open Source for Business, a go-to handbook for open source licensing in commerce.

Before her law career, Heather was a programmer of business applications and systems and a professional musician.
