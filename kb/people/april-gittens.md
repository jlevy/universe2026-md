# April Gittens

[Microsoft](../organizations/microsoft.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2025 as Principal Cloud Advocate, AI, Microsoft.

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

April is a developer advocate, educator, and creative technologist who excels at making emerging tech—especially AI—feel approachable and actionable. With a track record of leading global skilling initiatives, managing technical teams, and creating award-winning content across AI and Mixed Reality, she brings both strategic insight and clarity to every project and presentation. 

In her current role at Microsoft, April helps AI engineers build, test, and deploy smarter with developer tools like Visual Studio Code. She's also the author of two Wiley-published books—"Bite-Size Python" and "Visual Studio Code for Python Programmers"—both designed to help learners turn confusion into confidence. Outside of work, April is an electronics lover, serial crafter, and proud poodle mom, blending creativity and curiosity into everything she does.
