FDA & Alliance Lead, [Onepoint](../organizations/onepoint.md)

At Onepoint, he works at the intersection of enterprise architecture, cloud transformation, and AI-powered engineering, helping teams modernize how they build and deliver software with GitHub, Microsoft Azure, and modern DevOps practices. 

His work focuses on adopting the right technologies in a pragmatic way, from platform modernization and developer productivity to GitHub Copilot adoption, agentic AI, governance, and FinOps. Romain is also actively involved in technical communities, client workshops, enablement initiatives, and strategic technology partnerships, where he enjoys making advanced topics accessible and actionable for both technical and business audiences.
