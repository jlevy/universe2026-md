# Jelena Laketić

[EPAM Systems](../organizations/epam-systems.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2024 as Distinguished Engineer, Thomson Reuters.

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Jelena joined Thomson Reuters as the first Distinguished Engineer in March 2022. Detail-oriented engineering professional and strategic technologist with 24 years of experience, Jelena created and launched the Distinguished Engineer program for Thomson Reuters in May 2022. The program is designed to recognize Thomson Reuters’ outstanding technologists, establish a technical career path, and strengthen their engineering culture. 

As a result of the Distinguished Engineer program, following the rigorous selection process designed by Jelena, she was joined by the first cohort of Distinguished and Principal Engineers in 2023. Drawing upon her passion for innovation, the culture of diversity, cross-functional collaboration and engineering excellence, Jelena also developed a global AI hackathon for Thomson Reuters, an annual event first held in September 2023.

Before joining Thomson Reuters, Jelena was a distinguished engineer with UBS, Switzerland’s largest global universal bank. There she spent 15 years in a variety of roles, largely in creative, service-oriented roles specializing in client experience, applied innovation, and strategic topics. Those roles allowed her to blend her technical, organizational and people skills, shaping her into the technologist she is today. In addition to UBS, Jelena’s professional experience also includes roles with Siemens and other tech firms in Switzerland. 

Jelena's passion extends beyond technology, she is deeply committed to fostering the growth of young minds and advocating for women’s empowerment across industries. Engaged in various initiatives over the years, she consistently brings value to these causes. You might also be able to catch Jelena on "Gimme a Beat," her monthly radio show, where she is more commonly known as "DJ Babymonster."
