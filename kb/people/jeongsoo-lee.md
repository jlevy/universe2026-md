# Jeongsoo Lee

CodeQL Analysis Engineer, GitHub

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Jeongsoo is a CodeQL analysis engineer at GitHub, who is passionate about program analysis from both sides: theoretical and practical. He has a background in static analysis from studying abstract interpretation, as well as ones based on Datalog, whose parts can be found in the paper published on ICSE 2020: "SAVER: Scalable, Precise, and Safe Memory-Error Repair." He's been applying this knowledge by working with world-renowned players in the software industry to directly enhance their security from the earliest stages of development. In the day-to-day, his work includes designing and writing custom analysis in CodeQL and enabling customers to write queries on their own. After work, Jeongsoo can be seen ripping his ever-growing collection of rock music CDs.
