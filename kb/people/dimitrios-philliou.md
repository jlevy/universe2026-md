[Fivetran](../organizations/fivetran.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2024 as Product Manager, Copilot Extensions, GitHub.

Dimitrios is a product manager at GitHub, working on GitHub Copilot Extensions and the GitHub Marketplace. With eight years of experience spanning web development, go-to-market roles, and product management in the DevTools, AI, and fintech spaces, he brings a diversity of experience. A San Francisco native, Dimitrios still resides in the city with his family. He balances his work life with traveling, hiking, and trying new restaurants. Passionate about open source, Dimitrios recently contributed to Ersilia, a non-profit providing free machine learning models to streamline drug and disease research in low-income countries. In his spare time, he's building "Frog Dude" (a 2D wave shooter game) with his kids on GDevelop, playing pool, and working on a camper van buildout.
