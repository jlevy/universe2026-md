# Jen Peck

Senior Director of Engineering, Redfin

- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

Jen Peck is a Senior Director of Engineering at Redfin where she leads the team responsible for developer platforms, experience, and tools. Additionally, she co-led moving Redfin.com to the cloud. Prior to Redfin, Jen held engineering leadership roles at Gravity Payments, Starbucks, and Nordstrom and had a previous career in finance. As a feminist and a member of the queer community, Jen cares deeply about diversity, equity, and inclusion in tech and advancing the careers of those around her. Jen graduated from the University of Wisconsin with a Masters Degree in Finance.
