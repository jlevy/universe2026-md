Arnaud is a digital solutions architect who transforms complex technical challenges into elegant, scalable architectures. With over 18 years of experience spanning enterprise solutions and startup innovation, he brings a unique blend of technical depth and entrepreneurial insight to every project. Currently serving as principal engineer at Bouygues Telecom while managing his own ventures, Codename, Warehouse and Enia, Arnaud has consistently delivered transformative solutions including generative AI-powered assistants, unified API gateways, and innovative proptech applications. 

His technical leadership has earned him recognition from Microsoft, who awarded his chatbot technology the first prize in 2017, and his proptech innovation was shortlisted at RENT the same year. A fervent advocate for developer experience and open source contributions, Arnaud approaches complex problems with a first-principles mindset. Beyond his technical pursuits, he's a passionate movie enthusiast and composer, bringing the same creativity and attention to detail to his personal interests as he does to architecting digital solutions.
