# Shuyin Zhao

VP, Product Management, GitHub

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Shuyin is VP of Product for GitHub Copilot, the world’s first at-scale AI developer tool. She started her career as one of the founding members of XBox Live in 2001 and has extensive experience shipping a broad portfolio of consumer and enterprise products at Microsoft. In recent years, she's become passionate about building AI products for the greater good of the society, which includes the launch of Copilot. Outside of work, she is a proud mom of two college girls, along with her supportive husband.
