# Micaela Eller

[InnerSource Commons Foundation](../organizations/innersource-commons-foundation.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2023 as Global Program Ops Engineer, IBM.

- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

Micaela is an accomplished global program leader and transformation expert. Her dream is to make the world better by developing programs that are innovative and creative, with organizational cultures that are infused with compassion, courage, and empathy. 

She’s worked in a variety of industries, from music and entertainment to biotechnology to software to AI, and, as a result, has developed the unique ability to see beyond differences and identify common threads to bring ideas and people together. Micaela is an engaging and adept storyteller. She uses her expertise as a strategist, polymath, trainer, teacher, speaker, exec coach, agile coach, innovator, and unconventional thinker to empower Global Program Teams to work together to solve big problems. 

She’s a sought-after speaker on organizational, cultural, and agile transformation, facilitation, scrum, and servant leadership. She also speaks about women’s issues, women in agile, and her own mental health journey.
