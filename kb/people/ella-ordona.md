# Ella Ordona

Senior Software Engineer, Nordstrom

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Ella is a software engineer who designs and builds systems that help developers move faster with less stress. With years of experience supporting platforms used by thousands of engineers, she brings both technical depth and a focus on usability to her work. At Nordstrom, Ella works on the Developer Tools team, where projects span CI/CD pipelines, developer portals, and cloud infrastructure.  Passionate about creating systems that empower engineers, Ella also mentors junior developers and champions standard practices that make teams more efficient and resilient. Outside of work, she’s a creative technologist and can usually be found running, lifting, or hunting down the perfect pair of shoes.
