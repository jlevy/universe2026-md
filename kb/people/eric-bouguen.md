# Eric Bouguen

Managing Director, Europe Apps and Infrastructure Lead, Accenture and Avanade

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Eric is a managing director, leading applications and infrastructure for Europe at Accenture and Avanade. With over 20 years of experience driving enterprise transformation, Eric leads strategic initiatives that combine Microsoft Azure, GitHub, and generative AI to modernize software delivery across large organizations. His work specifically focuses on accelerating development velocity, reducing cycle times, and enabling collaboration between humans and AI to unlock new engineering patterns.

Eric’s deep technical background and leadership across multiple geographies make him a trusted voice in enterprise modernization. He is passionate about helping teams adopt future-ready practices that scale innovation and delivery.
