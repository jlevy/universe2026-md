# Claire Stehley

Staff Technical Program Manager, GitHub

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Claire loves to thoughtfully question the status quo about how we get work done—and she’s not afraid to ask hard questions!  She's deeply focused on driving teams toward goals that generate impact, helping people figure out what they need to do to reach their goals, and communicating that complexity to senior leadership.

She is currently serving as GitHub’s TPM for Enterprise Adoption. Before that, Claire held roles in engineering, program management, and field services, across multiple industries that include energy, tech, streaming, and the public sector.

As a person with anxiety, Claire loves to share ways to work collaboratively on a human level with lots of different types of people with different needs and work styles. When she isn't helping teams or doing public speaking, she loves to rollerblade, cook, read, and play video games. She resides in Washington DC with her partner, Amie, and her pet pigeon, French Fry.
