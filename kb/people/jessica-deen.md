# Jessica Deen

Staff Developer Advocate, GitHub

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)
- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Jessica is a Staff Developer Advocate at GitHub. She previously worked for AWS and Microsoft, and spent a decade working as an IT consultant and systems administrator in enterprise environments across the San Francisco Bay Area.

Her background bridges the gap between those who build technology and those who rely on it, giving her a unique ability to connect with developers, IT professionals, and enterprise teams alike.

Outside of tech, Jessica is usually off the grid (or on the sidelines) with her wife, teenage daughters, and dogs. You're most likely to find her behind a camera at a flag football or wrestling event, or off camping somewhere fun.
