# Thomas Bissell

[Pfizer](../organizations/pfizer.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2025 as Sr Associate, Digital Manufacturing Solution Engineering, Pfizer Inc.

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Thomas is a GitHub organization owner and DevOps engineer for Pfizer Digital Manufacturing, specializing in data integration projects that support manufacturing operations. With seven years of software engineering experience, including five years dedicated to DevOps at Pfizer, Tyler maintains a large set of GitHub repositories for various projects. These projects leverage a wide range of technology frameworks, from Spring Boot Java microservices to React front-end/UI, as well as .NET and Python. Tyler is passionate about solving problems in innovative ways that enable highly scalable solutions to enterprise challenges. Outside his professional life, Tyler is a musician with over 20 years of experience playing a variety of instruments. He plays bass guitar in a metalcore band in New Jersey. Additionally, he is a technology enthusiast with a passion for homelab and PC building. As an Eagle Scout, Tyler is committed to leadership and excellence, always striving to set an example for his peers on how to improve and innovate in their work.
