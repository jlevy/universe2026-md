# Jocelyn Velarde

GitHub Campus Expert, [Tec de Monterrey](../organizations/tec-de-monterrey.md)

- **GitHub Universe 2026:** speaker (confirmed)

Jocelyn Velarde is a Robotics and Digital Systems Engineering student at Tecnológico de Monterrey and a GitHub Campus Expert for robotics community RoBorregos. She builds autonomous robots for RoboCup @Home and published LLM-based task planning research at MICAI 2025. Alongside her robotics work, she has interned at software companies such as Insulet as a cloud developer, and has worked for Major League Hacking as a Coach, Admissions Specialist, Streamer, and Technical Associate. Her research in neurotechnology began with a mind-controlled hand prosthesis presented at I-FEST, grew into MoBI research at the IUCRC BRAIN Center, and now focuses on intracortical brain-computer interfaces at the UC Davis Neuroprosthetics Lab.
