# Jacob DePriest

VP, Deputy CSO, GitHub

- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

Jacob DePriest is an executive security leader at a world class technology company who has created and led multiple programs throughout his career providing technical leadership at a company/agency level. He has served in a variety of roles as a developer, technical lead, and executive leader. Jacob is currently the VP, Deputy Chief Security Officer at GitHub where he is responsible for managing the company’s Security team - including securing GitHub’s infrastructure, products, and data - making GitHub the trusted home for all developers. Prior to GitHub, Jacob was a senior executive at the National Security Agency where he built and led the Developer Experience program, was the Agency’s executive sponsor for open source, and led a number of IT and security transformation initiatives. Having led both engineering and security teams, Jacob is passionate about security working with the developer flow and not against it. Outside of work, Jacob enjoys staying active, reading science fiction/fantasy, and hanging out with his wife and two boys in Maryland.
