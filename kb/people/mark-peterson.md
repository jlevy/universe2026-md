# Mark Peterson

[Docusign](../organizations/docusign.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2025 as VP, Engineering, Docusign.

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Mark is a VP of engineering at Docusign, where he's dedicated to crafting exceptional user experiences and building a front-end engineering ecosystem that empowers engineers. He's a co-inventor of 1FE, a system designed with a core philosophy to make it easy to check-in and ship good code and hard to merge bad code — promoting a culture of quality. 

Mark has championed a "shift left" approach to quality initiatives, integrating them with engineering-led DevOps. This philosophy means each engineer takes full ownership of their work, from development to production delivery. When he's not shaping the future of software, you can find him playing bass guitar with his band in Seattle or coaching his son's First Robotics team.
