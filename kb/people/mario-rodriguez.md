# Mario Rodriguez

Mario Rodriguez leads GitHub's product organization and the company's AI strategy. He previously led the GitHub Copilot product line, launching and scaling it across thousands of organizations and millions of developers, and led GitHub's Enterprise business, which serves more than 90% of the Fortune 100. He has also been responsible for GitHub's core developer productivity services including repositories, pull requests, and issues.

Mario joined GitHub following Microsoft's acquisition of the company, after more than a decade at Microsoft in engineering and product management roles spanning cloud services and developer tools. Earlier in his career he was a founding member of the team behind Xbox's Forza franchise. He founded a charter school expanding access to education in rural America.
