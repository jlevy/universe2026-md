John Swanson is the Director of Security Strategy at GitHub, serving as a strategic advisor to GitHub’s CSO/SVP of Engineering and Deputy CSO. He brings nearly 15 years of experience in security and risk. Previously, John has held roles leading incident response, threat detection, and threat intelligence, as well as corporate crisis management, product security strategy, risk assessment, and strategy and program planning. 

Outside work, John is, first and foremost, a dad to two wonderful children, a keen student of history and geopolitics, and a barbecue and cocktail nerd.
