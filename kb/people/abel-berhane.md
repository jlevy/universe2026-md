# Abel Berhane

Sr Service Delivery Engineer, [GitHub](../organizations/github.md)

- **GitHub Universe 2026:** speaker (confirmed)

Abel Berhane is a technology leader and engineer who helps organizations turn emerging AI capabilities into practical, scalable ways of working. In his work with GitHub Expert Services, he has led and contributed to enterprise initiatives involving GitHub Copilot adoption, developer enablement, agentic software development, and technical transformation. His work helps customers embed new tools effectively throughout the software development lifecycle. 

Abel’s background spans DevOps, cloud engineering, automation, and developer productivity. He has designed and delivered strategic planning engagements, technical workshops, maturity assessments, and enablement programs for developers, engineering leaders, and enterprise teams. 

Abel holds a master’s degree in information technology from the University of West Florida. He is particularly interested in the intersection of artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and the future of software engineering. Outside of work, he enjoys exploring emerging technology, 3D design, and game development, as well as turning creative ideas into something tangible.
