# William Salazar

Senior DevOps Architect, GitHub

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

William is a senior DevOps architect with GitHub's FastTrack team specializing in DevOps, application development, and cloud architecture. He has over 28 years of experience in the business and commercial software industry, serving in various capacities in architecting large-scale enterprise solutions and implementations at strategic enterprise and fortune 500 companies.
