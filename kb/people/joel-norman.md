Joel loves code — but loves working with people even more. During the pandemic, Joel co-founded the nonprofit Canton Coders, helping thousands improve their software skills and supporting over 100 people in landing new software jobs. Over the past two decades as a developer, he has built solutions for decommissioning chemical weapons, screening premature infants for appropriate medications, and helping the world’s largest brands accelerate value creation through software.

Today, Joel co-leads Build Guild at Microsoft, where he focuses on helping some of the company’s largest customers adopt GitHub Copilot at scale. He and his team accelerate software value creation by leading with kindness, empathy, and a blend of economics, programming, humor, and dad jokes.
