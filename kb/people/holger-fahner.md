# Holger Fahner

[Mercedes-Benz](../organizations/mercedes-benz.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2023 as Senior Manager IT, Mercedes-Benz Group AG.

- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

As Senior Manager at Mercedes-Benz Group AG, Holger Fahner heads the Developer Experience department, aiming to provide software developers and engineers with an exceptional working experience that helps them stay in the flow and work more productively. Armed with an M.Sc. in electrical engineering and over 30 years in the technology sector, Holger brings a wealth of experience to the table. His passion lies in translating technical innovations into tangible benefits for the company. Before joining Mercedes-Benz Group AG, he wore multiple hats, including roles as a security consultant and a research engineer, developing software in close collaboration with esteemed European telco providers. From his early days at the company, Holger began establishing the internal Kubernetes-based Container Cloud, driving its success. Simultaneously, he played a pivotal role in carving the company’s path toward the cloud, by leading the Cloud Native Transformation Initiative within the Enterprise IT at Mercedes-Benz Group AG. Off the clock, he's a dedicated CrossFit enthusiast, always exploring his limits, and an aspiring espresso connoisseur, busy practicing the art of brewing the perfect cup.
