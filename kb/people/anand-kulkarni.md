# Anand Kulkarni

[CoreStory](../organizations/corestory.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2025 as CEO, CoreStory.

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Anand is CEO and founder of CoreStory, a company that leverages AI-driven intelligence models to reimagine and accelerate traditional legacy migration and application maintenance projects. Since its founding, CoreStory has developed proprietary model ensembles and agentic workflows to document the business and technical insights locked away in legacy code. This increases project speed and reduces the risk of failure when maintaining or modernizing strategic applications. 

Anand has been named to Forbes Magazine’s list of Top 30 Entrepreneurs Under 30. A vibrant contributor to scholarship in the engineering, mathematics, and technology spaces, he has published over a dozen peer-reviewed papers in ACM, AAAI, and IEEE journals and conferences, covering human-level artificial intelligence systems, data annotation, machine learning training, and more.

Anand held a National Science Foundation PhD fellowship in mathematics. He holds degrees in Industrial Engineering and Operations Research, Mathematics, and Physics from the University of California at Berkeley.
