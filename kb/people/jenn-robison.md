# Jenn Robison

Principal Software Engineer, Imagine Pediatrics

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Jennifer is a seasoned software engineer and former director of engineering, bringing over a decade of experience to the tech industry. With a passion for AGILE methodologies, she champions daily pair programming, test driven development, continuous integration, and iterative delivery to ensure top-notch code quality. Jennifer's dedication to sharing best practices extends beyond her professional roles; she has shared her career journey at the Society of Women Engineers conference, inspiring others with her transition from an administrative assistant to software engineer. 

From traditional web development to cutting-edge mobile applications with React Native, Jennifer's diverse experience empowers her to mentor and coach at events like Stanford University's TreeHacks and Girls Who Code. Her commitment to excellence has been recognized in various forums, reflecting her dedication to advancing the tech community. Outside of work, Jennifer enjoys sharing her passion for software development with middle school students and indulging in her love for the outdoors in the beautiful Pacific Northwest.
