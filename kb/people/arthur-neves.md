# Arthur Neves

[SpaceXAI](../organizations/spacexai.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2024 as Staff Software Engineer, GitHub.

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Arthur has been an integral part of GitHub for nearly a decade, dedicating the last five years to the GitHub Advanced Security (GHAS) team. As one of the pioneering developers behind the launch of GitHub's first GHAS product, Arthur has played a significant role in the evolution of numerous security solutions. His early tenure at GitHub was marked by his contributions to database-related infrastructure, where he helped design and scale services to accommodate millions of customers worldwide.

Arthur is more than just a developer; he is a builder at heart, who thrives on writing code and innovating. For the past year, he has been the lead engineer for code scanning autofix, a project that has been both a professional focus and a passion. Beyond his professional endeavors, Arthur is passionate about playing tennis and strives to be on the court at least five times a week.
