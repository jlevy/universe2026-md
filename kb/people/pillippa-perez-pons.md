# Pillippa Pérez Pons

Software Engineer, Independent

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Pillippa is a software engineer and community builder from Chile, now based in the Bay Area. She cares deeply about making technical topics—like Git—a little less intimidating and a bit more fun. As a director at Niñas Pro and organizer of JSConf Chile, she helps create spaces where girls, women, and the broader Latin American tech community can learn, experiment, and grow. She’s spoken at React Summit in New York and the Atlanta Developers' Conference, always bringing her curiosity, warmth, and a good dose of humor. When she’s not working on code or community, you can probably find her crafting ideas for events, or hanging out with her cats, Corchito and Batman.
