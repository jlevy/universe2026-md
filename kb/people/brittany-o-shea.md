# Brittany O'Shea

Director, Product Management, GitHub

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)
- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

For the past decade, Brittany, director of Product Management at GitHub, has focused on building security products to help organizations navigate emerging threats. She is passionate about helping developers and security professionals develop better security strategies, so their applications thrive in today's complex digital world. Before joining GitHub, she worked in various roles at Veracode, RSA, and IBM Security, with the mission of supporting teams in raising their security posture. When she's not working, Brittany enjoys spending time with her family and learning new hobbies and recipes.
