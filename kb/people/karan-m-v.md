- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Karan is the international developer relations director at GitHub, where he works closely with developers across enterprises, startups, nonprofits, academia, and the open source community. He operates at the intersection of AI and open source, helping organizations and individuals accelerate software development with GitHub at the center of their workflows.

Karan represents GitHub’s developer and ecosystem strategy in India, playing a leading role in shaping how the company engages with one of the world’s fastest-growing developer communities. He has featured in major developer keynotes alongside Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella and GitHub CEO Thomas Dohmke, where he showcased GitHub’s products and shared the story of how AI and open source are transforming software development.

A hands-on developer himself, Karan writes code daily and brings a unique blend of technical depth, ecosystem experience, and business insight. He is deeply passionate about the convergence of AI and open source, and how this intersection is shaping the next generation of developers and software.
