# Avanish Pathak

Sr. Principal Engineer, Docusign

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Avanish is a senior principal engineer at Docusign, where he identifies opportunities for improving products and experiences. Through 1FE, Avanish has enabled a distributed, self-serve, automated, and plug-and-play model of experience development that amplifies and builds upon the best practices in the industry. The platform allows for complete end-to-end delivery of enterprise-compliant software at speed — from idea and design conception to delivery — alongside ongoing monitoring and tracking enabled by centralized alerting systems. Outside of work, he dabbles in automation-enabled gardening, enjoys hiking, and rocks out on the drums.
