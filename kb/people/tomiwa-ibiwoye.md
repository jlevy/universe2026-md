# Tomiwa Ibiwoye

[Xi](../organizations/xi.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2025 as Co-founder, HacktivLabs.

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Tomiwa Ibiwoye is a full-stack software engineer and creative technologist with over a decade of experience building products that bridge technology and storytelling. From designing Africa’s first full-featured procurement software to leading immersive AI and AR experiences for global events like The Hundred, Tomiwa brings a unique blend of technical depth and imaginative thinking to every project.

He’s the mind behind high-impact applications for WE ARE XI and the IUF Group, and the principal architect of long-term installations like the Museum of Infinite Realities (MOIR), where he developed personalized, RFID-driven interactions that have generated over £150,000 in revenue.

Tomiwa has been featured in The Guardian Nigeria for his innovations in AI, won the Eko Heritage Award for Innovation, and recently appeared on national TV to discuss the future of AI development in Africa. When he’s not shipping code or strategizing for clients, he’s deep into flight simulation — so much so that he built a YouTube setup with full flight controls.

Whether working across Laravel, React, Node.js, or hardware integrations, Tomiwa is passionate about helping teams scale smartly and work more humanely — with GitHub at the center of it all.
