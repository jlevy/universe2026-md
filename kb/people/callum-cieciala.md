VP, Developer Experience, [Galaxy](../organizations/galaxy.md)

He oversees the company’s GitHub footprint across more than 15 organizations and 1,200 repositories. Over the past year, he has gone from cautiously assigning two-point Jira tickets to AI agents to launching hundreds of autonomous pull requests across the organization in a single morning, while building the production tools that made it possible. 

As VP of Developer Experience, Callum is part of the team responsible for CI/CD, GitHub governance, and developer tooling across Galaxy’s engineering organization. He has spent more than a decade building developer platforms and infrastructure. Outside of work, he enjoys live music and winning jackpots at the arcade with his son.
