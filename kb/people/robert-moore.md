# Robert Moore

Managing Director, Architecture & Engineering, Citi

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Robert has held many different positions in his 24 years with Citi, from applications development, large infrastructure team management, architect, and senior engineering lead. Robert currently leads the Developer Services organization within the CTO, providing software engineers frameworks, services, and best practices for software development across the enterprise. Robert is leading many modernization and transformational programs to include the introduction of Generative AI assistants for developers within Citi. This includes GitHub Copilot. Robert is a veteran of the U.S. Army, sits on advisory councils for the University of South Florida College of Engineering, Google Apigee, and is the executive sponsor for the Tampa Generations Network. He enjoys traveling, particularly to ancient sites across the western hemisphere.
