# Oscar Valenzuela

Principal Open Source Engineer, Amazon

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Oscar is a principal open source engineer at Amazon with over two decades of experience in software development, open source, and supply chain security. He drives technical strategy and leads initiatives to automate open source compliance at scale, with a current focus on researching supply chain risks from AI models and building agentic AI workflows for adaptive automation.

He has authored compliance tools to detect risks in AI-generated code and published extensively on AI security and legal issues. An advocate for Free and Open Source Software, Oscar serves on the boards of the OpenChain and Yocto projects and advises Software Heritage. In his spare time, Oscar slips into a secret open source identity by mixing his four Latin American names to contribute without worrying about recognition.
