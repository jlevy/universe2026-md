# Vincent King

Head of Secure Cloud Transformation, Bank of England

- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

Vince King is Head of DevSecOps for Cloud Transformation at the Bank of England. With more than 20 years’ experience working on software development projects both large and small, he is a reformed developer, SDLC advocate, and secure coding trainer. As a leader of Agile working practices within the Bank, Vince also has experience in supporting awareness and education programmes.

Vince has practical expertise in strategic leadership across multiple cyber security disciplines, including, incident response, risk management, vulnerability management, and Cloud transformation. He is a CISSP and CITP, and is passionate about mentoring others to achieve recognition for their work through engagement with the British Computer Society.

With his 20+ years of experience, Vince has developed, run, and presented courses on the OWASP Top 10, Secure Development methods, and DevSecOps Foundations and Best Practices, as well as being a regular speaker at conferences both in the UK and internationally.
