# Jon Clements

DevOps & Pipeline Product Manager, [Chevron](../organizations/chevron.md)

- **GitHub Universe 2026:** speaker (confirmed)

Jon Clements has led Chevron’s CI/CD DevOps team for eight years. He oversees the organization’s use of Azure DevOps and led its migration to GitHub. With more than 20 years of software experience, including roles at ExxonMobil and Boeing, Jon has spent the past 13 years at Chevron. He also contributed to Chevron’s migration of on-premises applications to the cloud and is now a primary contact for cloud adoption and modern software development practices. 

Jon also supports Chevron’s security strategy by selecting static and dynamic application security testing tools and helping design the company’s future approach to secure CI/CD. Outside of work, he enjoys board games and holds a Guinness World Records title for participating in the largest group to play a single continuous board game.
