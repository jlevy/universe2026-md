# Jeff Doolittle

Distinguished Engineer, Principal Architect, [Trimble](../organizations/trimble.md)

An architectural strategist and systems thinker, Jeff Doolittle is a Distinguished Engineer and Principal Architect at Trimble who focuses on transforming enterprise bottlenecks into seamless developer experiences. With more than two decades of experience in software design, he now pioneers AI-native workflows to drive innovation at enterprise scale. 

Jeff is a recognized leader and active voice in the engineering community, having served as a host on Software Engineering Radio and appearing on shows such as The Engineering Career Coach. When he isn’t using GitHub Copilot as a force multiplier to clear massive governance backlogs without writing a single line of code, Jeff loves exploring the intersection of human psychology and software design.
