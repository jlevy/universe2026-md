# Randy Pagels

[Xebia](../organizations/xebia.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2025 as Principal Trainer, Xebia USA.

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

As Principal Trainer at Xebia USA and a Microsoft MVP in Developer Tools, Randy focuses on expanding GitHub and AI training offerings and strengthening his team's ability to deliver them. He designs and delivers practical programs covering GitHub Copilot, Actions, Advanced Security, Prompt Engineering, and more. His courses are built around real-world scenarios and offered in flexible formats — from in-person workshops to on-demand content — to meet learners where they are. Randy is committed to maintaining high-quality standards, supporting trainer growth, and helping teams confidently adopt modern DevOps and AI-driven development practices.

Previously, Randy spent over 17 years at Microsoft, working closely with the Azure Engineering team in Redmond and helping to scale FastTrack for Azure globally. He regularly presents at conferences and events on topics including GitHub, DevOps, IoT, and Automated UI testing. His passion for clear, engaging, and actionable training has been a constant throughout his career.
