# Greg Padak

[GitHub](../organizations/github.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2025 as Director of Product, Enterprise, GitHub.

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Greg is a longtime Hubber and full-time dad with deep roots in developer tools. He began his career developing device drivers for broadcast equipment in 2008, gaining extensive experience in virtualization and open source build systems before eventually joining GitHub as a solutions engineer. 

After several years helping people adopt and expand their GitHub Enterprise usage, Greg found his calling in connecting engineers to customers and building impactful, enterprise-ready capabilities. He is now a Director of Product Management for GitHub Enterprise, specializing in Identity and Access Management. Greg's main drive is helping others, and bringing context and building coalitions around common causes to leave things better than he found them.
