# Shruti Corbett

Senior Director of Enginering, GitHub

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

As Senior Director of Software Engineering at GitHub, Shruti drives large-scale software development and customer solutions that improve efficiency and deliver measurable business impact. With more than 25 years of experience at GitHub, Microsoft, and IBM, she is recognized for managing global teams and has quickly earned “trusted advisor” status with clients and stakeholders.

Beyond her professional roles, Shruti actively mentors and develops emerging leaders in technology, fostering a culture of continuous learning and innovation. She holds an MBA in Management from the University of Florida and a BBA in Computer Information Systems from Florida Atlantic University.

Outside of work, Shruti enjoys being around people — whether she’s mentoring future technologists or running races (she has completed a half marathon each quarter since 2020 and ran her first marathon in NYC in 2022!).
