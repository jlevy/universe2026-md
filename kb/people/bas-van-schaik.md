# Bas van Schaik

[1Password](../organizations/1password.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2025 as Senior Director of Product, GitHub.

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Bas is one of GitHub’s senior directors of product, working on application security and AI. He focuses on helping the open source community and GitHub customers reduce risk through fast remediation of security issues at scale—assisted by AI and technologies like CodeQL. To do that, he works on GitHub products like GitHub Advanced Security, Code Security, Code Scanning, and Copilot Autofix.

He lives in Oxford, UK, with his wife, daughter, and two cats. When given the opportunity, he will chew your ear off about good single malt Scotch or the maintenance of vintage VW camper vans. In a past life, he obtained a Ph.D from the University of Oxford and published research on algorithms for reachability and compression of large-scale graph data, paleolatitude studies, and machine learning algorithms for correlated probabilistic data.
