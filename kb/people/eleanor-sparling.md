# Eleanor Sparling

[GSK](../organizations/gsk.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2024 as Principal Data Scientist, GSK.

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Eleanor is a versatile data scientist with Master's degrees in both Physics and Bioinformatics, bringing a unique blend of expertise to the field of clinical trial reporting. Currently at GlaxoSmithKline (GSK), Eleanor has consistently been at the forefront of innovation, playing a pivotal role in pioneering experiments that reshape clinical trial reporting workflows. Her experience includes designing knowledge graphs to reimagine the flow and reuse of data, which gained recognition in the finals of the UK IT Industry Awards.

Eleanor has contributed significantly to the design, development, and deployment of GSK's cutting-edge statistical computing environment, powered by Domino Data Lab. In particular, Eleanor led the design and implementation of GitHub workflows while responsible for large-scale, seamless adoption and utilization of GitHub across the Biostatistics department.

Outside of work, Eleanor can be found hiking, swimming, and apologizing for her overly friendly black lab, Inka.
