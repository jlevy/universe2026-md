# Ellie Bennett

[GitHub](../organizations/github.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2025 as Senior Product Manager, GitHub.

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Ellie is a senior product manager at GitHub, where she works on Copilot Chat. She began her career at IBM Watson and has since built software across education, government, and biotech startups. With over a decade of experience in product management, her focus has consistently been on creating tools that help professionals do their jobs more efficiently.

Outside of work, Ellie organizes a national book club that creates space for women in tech to expand their knowledge through curated reading and conversations with industry leaders. The group has several hundred members and has hosted guest speakers like Annie Duke and Teresa Torres. She lives in Philadelphia with her husband and enjoys reading, board games, and hiking trips to national parks.
