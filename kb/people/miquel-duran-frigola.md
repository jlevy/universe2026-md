# Miquel Duran-Frigola

Co-Founder and Lead Scientist, Ersilia Open Source Initiative

- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

Miquel holds a PhD in computational biology and is interested in AI. He has authored over 40 publications on a broad range of topics, from theoretical chemistry to medical data analysis. Currently, he serves as the Co-Founder and Lead Scientist at the Ersilia Open Source Initiative. With Ersilia, he hopes to bring his data science skills to underserved countries, especially in Sub-Saharan Africa. Miquel is an advocate for open science. In his free time, he devotes himself to fiction writing.
