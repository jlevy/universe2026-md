# Dr. Holger Kasinger

[ZEISS Group](../organizations/zeiss-group.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2024 as Head of Digital Experience Platforms, Carl Zeiss AG.

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Holger brings more than two decades of experience in information technology across multiple industries. He is currently VP of Corporate IT at Carl Zeiss AG, a pioneer in optics and optoelectronics, where he's working to digitally transform the business. 

Prior to ZEISS, Holger was the head of Digital Solutions and Technologies at Germany's largest municipal utility company, where he actively helped shape its energy and mobility transition. As a computer scientist, he's never lost his passion for high-quality software engineering. Holger was awarded the best doctoral thesis of the year by the University of Augsburg and has passed on his wealth of knowledge in many publications and presentations at scientific conferences, lectures, and industry meetings. Holger is also a passionate soccer enthusiast, so whenever the opportunity arises, he enjoys kicking the ball himself or exploring new ski resorts around the world.
