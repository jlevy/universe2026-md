Alex is a senior software engineer at Red Hat in Raleigh, NC, where he helps build the Ansible Automation Platform Hub. Whether he's engineering enterprise automation solutions, getting lost in speculative fiction, or crafting imaginative worlds in Dungeons & Dragons, Alex is passionate about creating powerful and collaborative experiences. He is a dedicated advocate for creating inclusive spaces where everyone's voice is heard. Away from the screen, you might find Alex deep in a board game with his partner or adding a new piece to his ever-growing fountain pen collection.
