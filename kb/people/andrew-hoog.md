# Andrew Hoog

Founder & Board Member, NowSecure

- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

Andrew Hoog is a developer, mobile security and forensics researcher, and co-founder of NowSecure. He is also a testifying expert witness, author of two books on mobile forensics for Android and iOS, and holds three patents in the areas of forensics, security, and data recovery. 

Recently, Andrew has developed various technical tutorials and YouTube videos about mobile development, DevOps, and automated security testing. Outside of work, he enjoys running, reading science fiction, traveling to new places, drinking great wine, and pausing to enjoy life.
