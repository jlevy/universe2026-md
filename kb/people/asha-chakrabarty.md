# Asha Chakrabarty

VP, Product Management, GitHub

- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

Asha Chakrabarty is the Vice President of Product Management for Security & Compute products at GitHub. Prior to joining GitHub, Asha was at Amazon Web Services (AWS) for 9+ years and wore multiple hats. Most recently at AWS, she led a global team of GTM and Product Specialists for AWS’ Container Services portfolio. Over the past 16+ years, her experience spans cloud computing, software development, modern application architectures, infrastructure design, and developer tools. Asha is based out of Austin, Texas where she spends her spare time with family, attempting to become a wine and dark chocolate connoisseur, and honing her novice photography skills.
