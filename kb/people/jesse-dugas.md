# Jesse Dugas

[Meta](../organizations/meta.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2023 as Senior Accessibility Program Manager, GitHub.

- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

Jesse Dugas is an accessibility and user experience professional with over a decade of experience in digital workspaces serving up to one billion daily active users. He currently leads program management for accessibility at GitHub. Jesse has worked in a variety of digital roles with an accessibility focus, including user experience design, user research, and front-end web and app development. 

Prior to GitHub, Jesse held various roles in accessibility at TikTok, Boston Scientific, and a variety of startups. He has also guest lectured on accessibility and user experience at his post-graduate alma mater, Harvard Extension School. He is passionate about ensuring people of all abilities can feel empowered in all that they do. When he’s not at work, Jesse enjoys hiking, traveling, board games, and playing basketball.
