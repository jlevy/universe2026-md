# Jeff Keyes

[Allstacks](../organizations/allstacks.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2025 as Field CTO, Allstacks.

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Jeff is Field CTO at Allstacks Intelligence Engine, the industry's first AI-native platform that makes traditional engineering dashboards obsolete.

With over 30 years of experience at Microsoft, Atlassian, Kaseya, and as Chief Architect at Captura Software (acquired by Concur), Jeff brings a rare combination of experience to the table: he's built complex platforms and positioned them for market impact.

That dual perspective drives his core conviction: engineering leaders shouldn't spend time investigating what's happening—they should spend it making decisions. Jeff helps CTOs and VPs of engineering escape the "investigate-analyze-execute" trap by leveraging Allstacks' proprietary semantic data fabric to surface delivery risks and optimize value stream flow across the entire software delivery lifecycle. As AI coding assistants reshape how teams build software, leaders need new intelligence to understand where engineering investment actually flows—shifting from operational firefighting to strategic impact.
