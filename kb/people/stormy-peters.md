# Stormy Peters

[Amazon Web Services](../organizations/amazon-web-services.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2023 as VP, Communities, GitHub.

- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

Stormy Peters is VP of Communities at GitHub, where she leads the teams responsible for creating programs and services structured to enable online creators, developers, and open source communities.

Throughout her career, Stormy has been passionate about open source software and has worked to educate companies and communities on how open source software is changing the software industry. Prior to GitHub, Stormy was the Director of the Open Source Programs Office at Microsoft, enabling 30,000+ developers to consume and contribute to open source effectively. She has worked at large organizations, started nonprofits, and led open source foundations. She currently serves on the Linux Foundation Board of Directors.
