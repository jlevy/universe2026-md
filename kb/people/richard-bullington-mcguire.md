# Richard Bullington-McGuire

[Modus Create](../organizations/modus-create.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2024 as Distinguished Engineer, Modus Create.

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Richard is a software architect and DevOps practitioner with more than 32 years of professional experience in information technology. He has operated Internet services continuously since 1995, when he established The Obscure Organization. He recently worked closely with Wayfair to move over 15,000 repositories to GitHub Enterprise Cloud.

Richard works at Modus Create, Inc. as a Distinguished Engineer, is a member of IEEE and the ACM, and leads technology operations for Freezing Saddles, the premier winter cycling competition for the greater Washington, DC area. He is the co-author of Docker for Developers from Packt publishing, published in 2020.

You can find him on GitHub, Bluesky, and Threads as "obscurerichard."

Richard lives in Arlington, VA with his wife and four children.
