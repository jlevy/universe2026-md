# Kean Hankins

DevSecOps Strategist, GitHub

- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

Kean Hankins works in AppSec at GitHub, and began in the AppSec space in the late 1990s launching sales for one of the first WAF (AppShield) and DAST tools (AppScan). Kean has industry work experience in both manufacturing and pharmaceuticals but have spent the majority of my career in software.  Some of his work focus areas are in Software Supply Chain Security, IoT Security and Business Intelligence for Security.  On a personal level, he works on multiple side projects in software project management and spends his spare time traveling with my wife and exercising at a crazy level!  Currently, Kean is doing research on the impact of AI on Application Security and measuring AI's impact on DevSecOps. Kean is based in Chapel Hill, NC.
