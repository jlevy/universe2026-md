# Eric Sondhi

[Arm](../organizations/arm.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2024 as Sr. Manager, IoT Line of Business, Arm.

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)
- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

Eric works closely with Arm's Partners and ecosystem (E.g.,  GitHub) to bring Arm's technology, tools, and to software developers to enable them to deploy their applications everywhere on Arm. 

As senior manger of Arm's Developer Marketing Strategy, Eric has worked with friends at GitHub for several years to establish and develop our technology partnership, and bring  integrations with GitHub Actions to market.

In previous go-to-market, product management, and sales engineering roles at Arm and in Arm's ecosystem, Eric has collaborated with Arm's lead partners, SoC designers, and software developers around the world to employ state-of-the-art simulation, modeling, and virtual prototype solutions for early SoC architecture and design, early software development, and system & software performance analysis. 

Eric is also a life-long lover of musicianship and has executive produced several film, video, music, and audio projects. He also produced, recorded, and engineered both live and studio productions for musicians local to the Boston Area for over 20 years.
