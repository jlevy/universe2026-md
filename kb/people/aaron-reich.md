# Aaron Reich

Chief Technology Officer, [Avanade](../organizations/avanade.md)

- **GitHub Universe 2026:** speaker (confirmed)
- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Aaron Reich is Chief Technology Officer at Avanade, where he leads research and development, AI products, and the company’s internal AI transformation. He also practices the fine art of nudging innovation into every corner of the business. His love of technology began when he was young, with his family’s Apple IIC and too many after-school hours in the computer lab. 

Aaron has a soft spot for startups and spends time coaching and mentoring entrepreneurs. He previously served as board president of The Healing Center in Seattle and was a devoted road runner until the arrival of his twin boys changed his schedule, but plans to make a comeback. Aaron holds a BA in business from the College of William & Mary and lives in Seattle.
