She has a proven track record of making complex tools feel usable and human, and now works with some of the largest enterprise customers in EMEA to make AI a true peer programmer: one that supports developers everywhere. She’s passionate about sharing knowledge and making others successful — most recently doing so at the Microsoft AI Tour in Cologne — helping even the skeptical fall in love with GitHub, bit by bit. Marie once helped run a cybersecurity book club, believes good documentation is a form of kindness, and rarely leaves the house without a paperback (or two) in tow.
