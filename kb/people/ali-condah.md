# Ali Condah

[GitHub](../organizations/github.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2024 as Sr. Director of Certification, GitHub.

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Ali is the senior director of Accreditation, Certifications & Enablement (ACE) at GitHub. In this role, he leads the Public Learning, Partner Readiness, and Certification programs. In FY24, the ACE team awarded over 7K and trained over 15K GitHub partners, and over 2M people completed GitHub modules in Microsoft Learn. 

Ali worked in enablement at Amazon Web Services (AWS) for over five years before joining GitHub in March of 2023. At AWS, he founded the Learning Design and Onboarding (LD&O) team for the Public Sector and Regulated Industries division, managing central enablement programs like Onboarding, Ongoing Development, Leadership Development, and Early Career Enablement.

Prior to AWS, Ali spent eight years in private equity-owned companies. Most recently, he developed the first partner enablement and certification program for Gemalto Thales Enterprise and Cybersecurity division. Before that, he created enablement programs for Social Solutions, CLEAResult, and ZIPS Franchising. Earlier in his career, he spent 10+ years in sales and marketing roles for telecommunications and entertainment companies, including AT&T, T-Mobile, and Universal Music Group.
