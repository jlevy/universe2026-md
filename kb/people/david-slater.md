# David Slater

Director, Research, [GitHub](../organizations/github.md)

- **GitHub Universe 2026:** speaker (confirmed)

David Slater is a Research Director at GitHub Next, where he explores the future of software development by building it. His work draws on machine learning, algorithms, security, and mathematical modeling, with a lingering fondness for the days when every problem called for a bespoke neural network. Before GitHub, he was a Principal Investigator on multiple DARPA research programs spanning machine learning on code, AI security and privacy, and cybersecurity. He earned bachelor’s and master’s degrees in electrical engineering at the University of Washington without ever touching a soldering gun (sorry, Weird Al). Outside work, he’s usually nerding out with bartenders over obscure cocktail ingredients or dancing Lindy Hop, tango, and blues.
