# Amanda Silver

CVP, Apps & Agents + 1ES GM, Microsoft

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)
- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Amanda has long shaped the future of software development at Microsoft, building tools, platforms, and ecosystems such as Visual Studio, VS Code, .NET, TypeScript, and the Azure developer experience and app platform. Today, as Head of Product for Apps and Agents, she oversees the Azure developer experience, Foundry agent experiences, observability, knowledge tooling, and forward deployment. As GM for 1ES, Amanda drives the evolution of Microsoft’s internal engineering systems, enhancing developer productivity while strengthening security, compliance, and agility. At the heart of both roles is her vision to reimagine the software development lifecycle as a continuous, agentic engineering system that transforms how software is built and how apps evolve—growing smarter and more capable with use.
