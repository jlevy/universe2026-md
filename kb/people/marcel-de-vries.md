# Marcel de Vries

Global MD & CTO, Xebia|Xpirit

- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

Marcel is the co-founder and Global MD & CTO of Xebia | Xpirit. He spends most of his time working with organizations to deliver software to production multiple times a day in a secure and compliant way. He helps organizations and leadership teams move the business needle by building an engineering culture. 

Marcel loves to learn new technologies. He shares his learnings at conferences and through training. Marcel has been awarded by Microsoft the Microsoft MVP award for more than 17 consecutive years and has also been a Microsoft Regional Director since 2008.
