# Michael YenChi Ho

[Crusoe](../organizations/crusoe.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2025 as Senior Product Manager, Microsoft.

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Yen‑Chi (Michael) Ho is a senior product manager on Microsoft’s Azure Core AI team. He leads product strategy for the Azure developer platform and AI‑assisted application modernization tooling — including GitHub Copilot app modernization, AI-powered experiences that help customers modernize and move applications to Azure while increasing developer velocity. Michael drives service portfolio strategy and positioning to ensure Azure’s offerings align with market demand and real customer needs, and he works closely with ISV and SI partners to scale the ecosystem.

Previously, Michael led Linux and OSS for Azure, shaping product strategy and technical marketing, and enabling Fortune 500 customers and partners to run large‑scale Linux solutions on Azure. He has deep experience collaborating with open source leaders such as Red Hat, SUSE, Ubuntu, Cloudera, VMware, Elastic, HashiCorp, and Confluent to land integrated solutions and GTM motions.

Outside of work, he’s a tech enthusiast and avid traveler who enjoys skiing and photography.
