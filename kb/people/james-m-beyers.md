# James M Beyers

[CVS Health](../organizations/cvs-health.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2025 as VP, Developer Experience, CVS Health.

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Jim works with CVS Health's DevEx team, with a mission to make CVS Health the best place to write quality software easily and securely. Drawing on his experience at Target and Capital One, Jim appreciates the challenge of connecting technology with business needs in practical ways.

Originally from Wisconsin, Jim moved to Minneapolis for college, where he earned a BA in Computer Science, an MS in Software Engineering, and an MBA from the University of Saint Thomas. His early roles in the hospitality industry helped him develop problem-solving skills and a focus on customer service—qualities he carries into his work today.

Outside of CVS, Jim enjoys spending time with his wife, Nora, and their two children, Amelia and Ben. Amelia plays guitar and bass, while Ben likes Pokémon and drums, keeping things lively at home. Jim’s interests include listening to music, going to the occasional concert with Nora, watching action and fantasy movies, and playing video games — especially “Age of Empires 2.” He values downtime with family and friends and believes in the power of small moments to make a big difference.
