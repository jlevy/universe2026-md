# Chris Griffing

Developer Advocate, GitKraken

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Chris is an avid Twitch streamer and YouTube creator with a passion for technical content. After a decade in software development, his passions led him to become a developer advocate at GitKraken, where he represents the interests of 30 million developers who rely on GitKraken’s DevEx platform. Chris is a familiar face on stage at many of Seattle’s tech meetups and at conferences, including the Manning Rust Conference, NeovimConf, and the React Summit US. Outside of tech, Chris loves to snowboard and would say that this is his happy place.
