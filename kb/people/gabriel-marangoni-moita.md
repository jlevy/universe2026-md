# Gabriel Marangoni Moita

[SAP](../organizations/sap.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2024 as GitHub SAP Product Owner, SAP.

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Gabriel is the product owner for GitHub@SAP and his team is responsible for the two central deployments of GitHub Enterprise Server (GHES) in SAP. Their GHES instances are among the largest in the world, with more than 50,000 active users, maintained with above 99.95% availability, allowing SAP to deliver software to more than 230 million users worldwide. Before this role, he began his career as a CI/CD automation developer and became the CI/CD lead for an SAP product. His experience as a user supports his decisions and leadership now as a product owner. Gabriel is a Brazilian living in Germany and enjoys playing RPG and gaming, as well as practicing traditional Chinese archery, highlighting his insatiable curiosity.
