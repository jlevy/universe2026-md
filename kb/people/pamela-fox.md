# Pamela Fox

Principal Cloud Advocate in Python, [Microsoft](../organizations/microsoft.md)

- **GitHub Universe 2026:** speaker (confirmed)
- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)
- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

Pamela is a cloud advocate in Python at Microsoft. She loves to learn, teach, and create. She’s been in the tech industry for 15 years, beginning with a role at Google as one of the company’s first developer advocates. She went on to become an early full-stack engineer at Coursera and spent many years afterward at Khan Academy, both as an engineer and the creator of computer programming content.

On the teaching front, Pamela taught computer science at UC Berkeley and volunteered in Bay Area classrooms as part of the TEALS, Girls Who Code, and CoderDojo organizations. She started the San Francisco chapter of Girl Develop It, where she taught dozens of web development workshops.
