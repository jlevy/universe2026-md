# Aaron Scofield

Senior Software Engineer, Prudential

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Aaron is a Philadelphia-based senior software engineer on the Enterprise DevOps team at Prudential Financial, where he delivers solutions to over 5,000 developers within the company. After earning his degree from Temple University, Aaron joined the new graduate rotational program at PGIM Fixed Income, immersing himself in the financial services industry. During his rotation with the Fixed Income DevOps team, he discovered his passion for automation and DevOps principles, which led him to transition to the enterprise-wide DevOps team. Currently, Aaron is focused on Prudential’s migration to GitHub Enterprise, and he is particularly proud of the repository migration engine he designed. If you see him around the conference, don’t hesitate to ask him about it!
