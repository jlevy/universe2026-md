# Farhan Thawar

[Shopify](../organizations/shopify.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2023 as Head of Engineering, Shopify.

- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

Farhan Thawar is VP and Head of Engineering at Shopify via the acquisition of Helpful.com, where he was co-founder and CTO. Previously he was the CTO, Mobile at Pivotal and VP of Engineering at Pivotal Labs via the acquisition of Xtreme Labs. 

Farhan is an avid investor and advisor to startups in Toronto and San Francisco, including being a mentor at  Y Combinator and First Round Capital. Before his current job, Farhan held senior technical positions at Achievers, Microsoft, and Trilogy. Farhan completed his MBA in Financial Engineering at Rotman and Computer Science/EE at Waterloo.
