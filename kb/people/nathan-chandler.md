# Nathan Chandler

Executive Manager of Engineering, Commonwealth Bank of Australia

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

With 20+ years of engineering & architecture experience, Nathan is focused today on providing the best tools, capabilities, and processes to support an ever-improving engineering experience for over 10,000 highly motivated engineers. As a leader of platform engineering at Commonwealth Bank of Australia (CBA), Australia’s largest financial institution, he drives a DevSecOps maturity mission to improve delivery velocity, software quality, and security in support of CBA’s bold customer NPS mission. Nathan remains ever curious and a passionate advocate for technology change and evolution. He is a key capability owner of CBA’s AI-powered engineering strategy to harness AI to improve all aspects of the software delivery lifecycle.  Nathan still loves the Sydney sunshine, after migrating from the UK in 2008, and whenever possible is out on a bike, on a hike, occasionally on the golf course, or regularly drinking great coffee.
