Carlin is a Senior Product Manager at GitHub, working on Dependabot and the software supply chain. With a unique background that combines data science and software engineering experience, she brings technical depth and user empathy to vulnerability management. In her role, Carlin leads efforts to make security integrate seamlessly into developer workflows, helping organizations prioritize what matters most. Outside of work, she is a board member of her alma mater, Rice University (go Owls!), and a member of the ThinkCyber Foundation. She also enjoys running and plays board games competitively.
