# Sumeet Shetty

Head of Tools India, SAP

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Sumeet is the head of Tools India | Product, Data and Technology at SAP, where his current focus is on bringing AI pair programming to over 15,000 developers. Since joining SAP in 2005, Sumeet has been part of various strategic initiatives, including the global rollout of lean and agile software development, design thinking, and business model innovation. He has been a speaker at SAP d-com, SAP TechEd, Microsoft ALM Days, the British Council, the India International Center, and the National Gallery of Modern Art.

Sumeet is also president of Literati, India's largest corporate book club, and has interviewed some of the world's finest authors, thinkers, and leaders. His passions include AI, leadership, art, design, literature, and public speaking. Harvard Business Review described him as “an exceptional leader and coach, recognized for his track record of building high-performance teams.”
