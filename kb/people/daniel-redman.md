# Daniel Redman

[GitHub](../organizations/github.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2025 as Senior DevOps Architect, GitHub.

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Daniel is a senior DevOps architect on GitHub’s FastTrack/ODST team, specializing in helping GitHub customers overcome their most complex challenges and ensuring their platform usage is tailored to their needs and aligned with best practices. Daniel joined GitHub in 2020, coming from the Azure DevOps organization at Microsoft, where his FastTrack team was originally formed. With over two decades of experience in software development, architecture, and automation, he has led transformative DevOps initiatives across numerous enterprise environments. Daniel is an active contributor to the DevOps community, speaking at regional tech meetups and mentoring aspiring software engineers through local mentorship programs.
Outside of work, Daniel is an avid outdoorsman who enjoys hunting, fishing, boating, hiking, and spending time at his camp near the Allegheny National Forest.
