# Paul Snider

CTO, [Provation](../organizations/provation.md)

- **GitHub Universe 2026:** speaker (confirmed)

Paul Snider is Chief Technology Officer at Provation Medical, where he leads technology strategy across the company's platforms and products. He brings two decades of healthcare technology leadership, with deep experience building and scaling enterprise clinical systems in highly regulated environments. 

Before Provation, Paul held executive roles at Cerner, helping scale one of the largest electronic health record platforms in the world, and later served as VP of Engineering at Netsmart, leading data, interoperability, and AI platforms supporting behavioral health and post-acute care. Paul has also shared his perspective on engineering leadership and applied AI at industry and partner events. He holds a BS in Computer Information Systems from Missouri State University and an MBA from Webster University.
