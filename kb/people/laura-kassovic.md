# Laura Kassovic

[Develocity](../organizations/develocity.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2025 as Technical Documentation Lead, Gradle.

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Laura is a technical writer at Gradle, where she specializes in documentation and developer education. She is passionate about bridging the gap between complex technical concepts and user-friendly documentation. Her experience spans from hands-on hardware development to crafting comprehensive guides that enhance developer onboarding and productivity.

Prior to her role at Gradle, Laura co-founded MbientLab and Beacon3D, companies focused on wearable technology and 3D-sensing solutions. Her work was recognized in the Arm Innovator Series, which highlighted her contributions to the development of Bluetooth-enabled sensor platforms and their applications in various industries.
