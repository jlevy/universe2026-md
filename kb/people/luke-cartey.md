Luke is a director in Expert Services at GitHub, responsible for GitHub's security-related services, including GitHub Advanced Security (GHAS) and CodeQL. Luke joined GitHub as part of the acquisition of Semmle in 2019, creator of CodeQL. Luke worked for Semmle for six years after completing his doctorate at University of Oxford in “Domain Specific Languages for Graphics Cards.” His interests include the application of static analysis at scale to solve real-world problems, and the effective deployment of code analysis tools that help improve the security of large organizations.
