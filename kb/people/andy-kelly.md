# Andy Kelly

VP of Research and Development, [Precisely](../organizations/precisely.md)

- **GitHub Universe 2026:** speaker (confirmed)

Andy Kelly is Vice President of R&D at Precisely, where he leads Platform Engineering, SaaS Foundation Services, and Product Security. His teams span SRE, pipeline engineering, developer experience, foundational AI services, and product security engineering. With a deep passion for DevOps and continuous delivery, Andy has spent more than two decades applying continuous improvement principles to engineering at scale—building the metrics, scorecards, and operational rigor that make engineering performance visible, measurable, and directly accountable to business outcomes. He has held senior technology leadership roles across the data and analytics software industry, scaling engineering organizations through sustained growth and M&A. Andy lives in Wigan with his wife and daughter. When he’s not interrogating his engineering health scorecard, he follows both rugby union and rugby league with great dedication.
