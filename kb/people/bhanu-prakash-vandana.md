# Bhanu Prakash Vandana

[Omnissa](../organizations/omnissa.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2024 as Senior Member of Technical Staff, Omnissa.

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Bhanu Prakash is more than just an SRE/DevOps engineer; he is an innovative architect of resilient cloud infrastructures. With a deep-seated expertise in critical system migrations, exemplified by his successful transition of his business unit from GitLab to GitHub, Bhanu Prakash is known for his ability to design and implement robust failover strategies. These strategies have proven to dramatically reduce team sizes while maintaining system integrity, exemplified by his reduction of engineering requirements from 25 to just five professionals.

His skill in applying technology effectively is evident from his lead on a major version database upgrade for critical services, managing to limit downtime to an exceptional two minutes. Additionally, Bhanu Prakash has crafted a pioneering GitHub workflow architecture that enables the creation and synchronization of multiple workflows across various teams, significantly streamlining the process and reducing the effort to just a few clicks.

Outside of his technical endeavors, Bhanu Prakash is an amateur poker player and a lover of adventurous outdoor activities. His passion for adventure is leading him to plan a solo skydiving jump for his next birthday.
