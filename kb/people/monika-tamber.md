# Monika Tamber

AVP, Application Security & Operational Governance, [Manulife](../organizations/manulife.md)

- **GitHub Universe 2026:** speaker (confirmed)

Monika is an award-winning cybersecurity and engineering leader with more than 20 years of experience across the financial services, telecommunications, and semiconductor industries. As Associate Vice President of Application Security at Manulife Financial, she leads a global team responsible for protecting customer data, funds, and capital across Global Wealth and Asset Management. Her team secures more than 800 applications used daily for retirement, retail, and institutional investments worldwide. 

Before joining Manulife, Monika served as Director of Security Governance at Payments Canada, where she helped secure and modernize Canada’s critical payments infrastructure that exchanges and settles billions of dollars each day. Prior to her cybersecurity career, she spent more than a decade in engineering, developing video algorithms and microchips that supported product launches across North America and Europe. She also led numerous engineering and security initiatives at Rogers Communications that enhanced the customer experience and expanded the company’s telecommunications footprint across Canada. 

Monika holds a degree in Computer Engineering from the Schulich School of Engineering and a master’s degree in Risk Management and Business Performance from the Schulich School of Business. She is a Professional Engineer and holds multiple certifications in information security. 

Her achievements include recognition as one of Canada’s Top 20 Women in Cybersecurity, the Diversity and Equity Alumni Award, finalist for the Top 25 Immigrant Award, and the Spotlight Award for her work as a Security Architect at the Bank of Montreal. 

Outside of work, Monika is passionate about empowering women and youth in engineering, technology, and cybersecurity. She is an active mentor and speaker on diversity and inclusion.
