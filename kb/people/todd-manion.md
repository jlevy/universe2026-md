# Todd Manion

VP, Product Management, GitHub

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

With over 25 years product experience, Todd has a keen sense for crafting transformative experiences that customers love. He was a high-energy product management and business leader at Microsoft, and repeatedly led v1.0 and next-gen product initiatives through the entire product lifecycle — from concept, vision, and strategy through go-to-market execution. 

Together with talented team members and partners on key projects for Windows, Azure, Xbox, OneDrive, and more, Todd has achieved critical market recognition and lasting results. In his current role leading the GitHub Enterprise Product team, Todd is a driving force, building GitHub’s next-generation enterprise cloud to power the world’s leading AI-powered developer platform.
