# Joe Bourne

[CINC Systems](../organizations/cinc-systems.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2024 as Staff Product Manager, GitHub.

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Joe is a staff product manager at GitHub with a mission to redefine the developer experience through the power of AI. He's passionate about empowering developers to innovate faster than ever with GitHub Copilot, aligning with GitHub’s goal of accelerating human progress through enhanced developer collaboration.

Over the past five years at GitHub, Joe has also focused on saving developers time, playing a pivotal role in integrating native CI/CD into GitHub Actions, where he honed his expertise in developer workflow automation. 

Prior to GitHub, he spent nine years at Microsoft, where he began his journey in developer experience by working on the Azure DevOps Git repository and Pipelines experience. His diverse background also includes working on customer-centric experiences like Windows, Windows Phone, and the Cortana personal assistant. Now, Joe is deeply invested in the potential of AI to both streamline development and drive innovation in unprecedented ways. Recognized for his customer obsession and personal excellence, Joe has received multiple Microsoft awards throughout his career. Outside of work, he is a father of four, a guitar player, a new chess student, avid video gamer, and is embracing a new passion for fitness.
