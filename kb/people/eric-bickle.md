# Eric Bickle

[Asurion](../organizations/asurion.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2024 as Sr. Principal Application Security Engineer, Asurion.

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

With over two decades of experience in the tech industry, Eric is always eager to share his passion for software development by demonstrating how security can be a creative and integral part of day-to-day coding activities. In his role as a senior principal security engineer at Asurion, he mentors and guides developers towards secure solutions.

With diverse experience in previous roles as an enterprise architect, software engineer, and systems administrator, Eric brings a unique and comprehensive perspective to both security and development. Over the years, he’s had the opportunity to design and implement a wide variety enterprise-scale solutions, such as cryptographic data protections, services that scale for millions of active users, and platforms for federal and state public health initiatives.

Away from his professional life, Eric's adventurous spirit often finds him exploring the wilderness of Western Canada, whether backpacking through the mountains or kayaking in the ocean. His love for exploration and facing challenges head-on echoes his approach to his work, showcasing his readiness to navigate uncharted territories and overcome new challenges.
