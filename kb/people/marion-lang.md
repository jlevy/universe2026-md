# Marion Lang

Principal Software Enigneer, GitHub

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Marion is a principal software engineer at GitHub, where she works on a variety of platform and infrastructure systems. Prior to GitHub, she worked on building and operating large-scale distributed systems at Google and Mozilla. She's also held various academic appointments where she taught distributed systems and software engineering, and has done both theoretical and applied research in the areas of distributed systems and distributed data processing. Marion lives in San Francisco with her partner and enjoys cooking, tinkering, microcontroller programming, running, and ice hockey.
