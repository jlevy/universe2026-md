# Kat Cosgrove

[VillageSQL](../organizations/villagesql.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2024 as Lead Open Source Advocate, Independent.

Kat is a lead developer advocate at Dell, focused on growing and nurturing open source through authentic contribution. Her specialties are creating approachable 101-level content and deep dives on the history of technology, with a focus on DevOps and cloud native. She's been involved in the Kubernetes project for years, particularly as part of the Kubernetes release team. She was the release lead for v1.30 Kubernetes and currently serves as a Kubernetes release team subproject owner and SIG Docs tech lead. When she's not doing open source or at a conference, she spends her time playing video games, watching horror movies, and reading science fiction. She lives in Scotland with her cat, Espresso, who is the real brains behind the operation and is ghostwriting all her tweets.
