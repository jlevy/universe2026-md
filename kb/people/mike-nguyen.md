# Mike Nguyen

Director of Solutions Engineering, Octopus Deploy

- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

Mike Nguyen is the Director of Solutions Engineering with Octopus Deploy—serving his technical sales team to enable organizations on their code-to-cloud journey.
After graduating from the University of Waterloo, he started his 20+ year career in the software space in development, and has since been fortunate to experience the evolution of software development—from the ways of waterfall at PTC, to the advent of agile at CA Technologies, to the dawn of devops at GitHub and HashiCorp.
As a lifelong technologist, what Mike cares about is the continuous improvement of all things.
Beyond software and continuous improvement, he is a husband and father who is a fan of food, fitness, and footwear!
