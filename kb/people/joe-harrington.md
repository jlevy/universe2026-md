# Joe Harrington

Senior Security Engineer, Principal Financial Group

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

With over a decade of experience writing and protecting software, Joe today often finds himself explaining his developer friends’ woes to security folks, and his security friends’ woes to developers. Moving from a role as a backend Java developer for a Fortune 200 company, to open-source developer at a major research university, Joe returned to the private sector six years ago as a DevOps champion and cloud platform engineer before landing in an application security role. In the process, he's learned that solving human problems is often harder–and more rewarding–than solving purely technical ones. Joe holds a CSSLP certification from ISC2, as well as GitHub certs in Advanced Security and Administration, which he picked up at Universe last year. When he's not enabling DevSecOps, automating things, or hunting for vulnerabilities in code, Joe can be found dancing heys and "Mad Robins” with his fellow English country dancers.
