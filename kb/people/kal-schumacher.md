# Kal Schumacher

Senior Cloud Engineer, Duke Energy Corp.

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Kal is a senior cloud engineer at Duke Energy. Together with his team, he has had the opportunity to lead the way in shifting the culture of their organization from "every team for themselves" to "we’re all in this together." Although he is an engineer by trade, Kal's passion lies in connecting people and helping large groups work together effectively. 

Living in the world of automation, Kal loves to strategize on the most efficient way to do everything, whether it’s complex migrations or how to fit the most dishes in the dishwasher (just ask his wife!), while ensuring the human connection is never sacrificed. In his free time, Kal loves to play trivia at breweries and is the captain of his intramural kickball team—showcasing his natural ability to lead and strategize in any situation.
