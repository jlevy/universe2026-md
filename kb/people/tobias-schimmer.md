# Tobias Schimmer

[SAP](../organizations/sap.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2024 as Head of Developer Experience, SAP.

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Prof. Dr. Tobias Schimmer is SAP's global head of developer experience. Previously, he served as a chief development expert for SAP's Industry Cloud business unit in charge of the global collaboration model and developer experience. Before this, he was principal product manager for SAP's Innovation Center in Newport Beach, which he helped establish in 2018.

Since joining SAP in 2009, Tobias has led various strategic initiatives, including the global rollout of lean and agile software development, design thinking, and business model innovation while piloting projects like SAP SportsOne and SAP Fashion Management. 

In addition to his role at SAP, Tobias is actively involved in research and teaching enterprise software innovation and software engineering. He is an honorary professor at the University of Mannheim and a lecturer and visiting researcher at the University of California, Irvine.

Aside from work, Tobias is passionate about fitness, his kids, and coaching young talent.
