# Cameron Hamilton

Creator, [Alliance AI](../organizations/alliance-ai.md)

- **GitHub Universe 2026:** speaker (confirmed)

Cameron Hamilton is an AI engineer, researcher, and founder of Alliance AI, with more than a decade of experience building applied AI systems. He created HALIE, a proactive AI assistant designed to anticipate users’ needs and help address them. His projects also include Endgame, an open-source machine learning library; the Parkinson’s Insight Engine (PIE); and Alliance Desk, an AI front-desk agent that helps businesses avoid missed bookings.

Cameron’s work explores how AI agents can become more capable and useful while keeping people meaningfully in control. As a public figure and content creator with a cross-platform audience of over 3.5 million, he connects technical ideas to everyday experiences, making AI accessible to builders, creators, and broader audiences. He believes that AI should expand what people can accomplish, helping them bring ambitious ideas to life while preserving human judgment, creativity, and agency.
