# Lelia Bray-Musso

[Socket](../organizations/socket.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2024 as Open Source Architect, Independent.

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Lelia is an open source enthusiast, automation aficionado, mentorship advocate, and former film major. As an open source architect at Cisco, she primarily focuses on improving Cisco's open source security posture while streamlining the management of numerous GitHub organizations. 

Prior to Cisco, Lelia served as a staff engineer and founding member of Wayfair's Open Source Program Office (OSPO), and led the Platform pillar. With over a decade of experience in the tech industry, spanning everything from e-commerce to enterprise security to consumer robotics, she's found herself continually drawn to the intersection between software and the people developing it. Her recent involvement in OpenSSF's Scorecard community has greatly accelerated her passion for secure software development and eagerness to contribute back to the project. Lelia is based out of Boston, but is known to spend time in Miami, FL. and will befriend at least one cat anywhere she travels.
