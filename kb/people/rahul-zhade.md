# Rahul Zhade

Senior Product Security Engineer, GitHub

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Rahul is a product security engineer at GitHub, where he is known for developing innovative solutions to some of engineering’s toughest security challenges and ensuring that GitHub’s products are delivered both quickly and securely without sacrificing usability. With over five years at GitHub, Rahul has played a key role in securing flagship projects and ecosystems like npm, GitHub Copilot, and GitHub Next. He is a recognized voice in the industry, having delivered talks on AI security for GitHub’s Open Source Fund and worked with international organizations such as the Norwegian Refugee Council to strengthen their platforms.

In the past year and a half, Rahul has been instrumental in driving the security vision for the Copilot suite, including the Copilot Coding Agent, GitHub Models, and Copilot Spaces, and has helped architect GitHub’s internal security framework for AI features, paving the way for secure and scalable AI development. Outside the world of security, Rahul channels his energy into running and boxing, embracing fitness as a way to challenge himself and stay balanced — whether he’s securing software, or chasing his next personal best.
