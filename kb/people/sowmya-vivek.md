# Sowmya Vivek

Director - Product, GitHub

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Sowmya is a seasoned product leader who thrives at the intersection of technology, business strategy, and customer impact. With over 20 years of experience, she has built and scaled enterprise-grade billing, payments, and monetization platforms that power global businesses.

As Director of Product at GitHub, Sowmya leads the vision and execution of GitHub’s billing and licensing systems. She has been instrumental in launching usage-based billing models that underpin AI monetization, while also driving simplification and modernization of the licensing platform. Her strength lies in translating complex infrastructure challenges into scalable, user-centric solutions.

Currently, Sowmya is focused on defining billing metrics and developing AI-driven billing use cases that improve operational efficiency and enhance customer experience.

Outside of work, Sowmya enjoys exploring DIY art projects and is a proud coffee enthusiast. She has perfected the craft of making South Indian filter coffee — something she’s always excited to share as an icebreaker!
