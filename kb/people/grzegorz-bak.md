# Grzegorz Bak

Head of Product Enablement, GitProtect.io

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Greg is the head of product enablement at Xopero Software S.A. and GitProtect.io, specializing in backup solutions for Azure DevOps, GitHub, Bitbucket, Jira, and GitLab. Over the past 14 years, he has held many roles at the company, steadily expanding his responsibilities and skills. As a world-class backup expert, he is also interested in algorithms and data encryption technologies. His interdisciplinary skills allow him to bridge the gap between advanced technology and business, explaining concepts so clearly that it's hard to remember ever not understanding them. Thanks to these unique abilities, Greg regularly gives interviews, appears on podcasts, and provides expert commentary.
