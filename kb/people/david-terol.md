# David Terol

Head of Software Engineering Excellence, Philips

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

David has been working together with software teams within Philips since early 2018 when he moved to the Netherlands from his native Spain. Since mid-2020, he is leading corporate initiative for Philips to become an InnerSource-as-default company and grow its Open Source public company profile. He is a strong advocate of open collaboration models like InnerSource based on high transparency, zero-wait, contribution over request and low friction principles to break artificial internal boundaries which jeopardize true value delivery to their customers on complex organizations.
