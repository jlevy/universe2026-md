# Sue Black

Professor of Computer Science and Technology Evangelist, [Durham University](../organizations/durham-university.md)

- **GitHub Universe 2026:** speaker (confirmed)

Professor Sue Black OBE is an award-winning computer scientist, technology leader and social entrepreneur, passionate about using technology to create positive change. A Professor of Computer Science at Durham University, she is best known for leading the successful campaign to save Bletchley Park and for founding initiatives including BCSWomen, #techmums and TechUPWomen, supporting women and underserved communities into technology careers.
Named one of Forbes’ Top 50 Women in Tech globally, Sue was awarded an OBE for services to technology and named Everywoman’s Tech for Good Award winner in 2025. She advises government and industry on technology and AI, speaks internationally, and is a strong advocate
for inclusive innovation. Sue is proud to serve the Worshipful Company of Information Technologists as a Court Liveryman.
