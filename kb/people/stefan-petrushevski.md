# Stefan Petrushevski

[GitHub](../organizations/github.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2025 as Senior Security Solution Architect, GitHub.

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)
- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

Build it, break it, fix it better! This is the guiding principle for Stefan, a seasoned cybersecurity professional with over a decade of experience across various industries. With a solid background in computer science, he specializes in security evaluation and research, passionately working to bridge the divide between security and engineering teams. 

Stefan is currently a senior security solution architect at GitHub, where he assists enterprise customers in evolving their application security programs with GitHub Advanced Security. He is also an active contributor to the security community, having co-authored tools, workshops, and talks presented at BlackHat, GitHub Universe, OWASP, and DevSecCon. 

Stefan is a proud member of the Zero Science Lab, which engages in independent security research. He also has a passion for music—a space outside of hacking where he can express his creativity.
