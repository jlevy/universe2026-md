Alex is VP of Applied AI at AMD, where he focuses on helping engineering teams put AI to work across the full software development lifecycle, from build systems to validation and delivery. With more than 20 years in software engineering leadership, he has built and scaled global development teams, led enterprise-wide source management and build system migrations, and driven large-scale transformations that make engineering teams more effective. Before moving into applied AI, Alex led software infrastructure at AMD and held various engineering roles at ATI Technologies. He earned his bachelor's in computer science from the University of Toronto and holds multiple patents in system design and AI.
