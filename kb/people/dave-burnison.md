# Dave Burnison

[GitHub](../organizations/github.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2024 as Senior DevOps Advocate, GitHub.

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Dave is a senior DevOps advocate on the GitHub and Azure DevOps teams. He is very passionate about DevOps and application modernization. Dave joined Microsoft in January 2016 and moved over to GitHub in 2020. Dave spent time as a consultant and in technical sales prior to joining the product group and has been focused on DevOps the entire time.

In 2024, Dave was awarded the Microsoft Executive Briefing Center’s (EBC) Performance Excellence award. The award winners for this category represent our best speakers based on feedback from customers, NSAT scores, and number of deliveries over the year. See http://aka.ms/DaveBurnison2024EBCAward. 

In 2022 Dave was awarded the Microsoft Executive Briefing Center’s (EBC) Distinguished Speaker award. Distinguished Speaker is the highest achievement Microsoft has at the EBC. This award recognizes speakers who embody all attributes of EBC and customer engagement best practices. See http://aka.ms/DaveBurnison2022EBCAward.
