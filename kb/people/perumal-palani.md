# Perumal Palani

Sr Staff Software Engineer, PayPal

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Perumal is a seasoned full-stack architect who transforms complex commerce challenges into elegant, scalable solutions. With nearly two decades of experience, he has deep expertise in web and mobile applications, module federation, APIs, and advanced web frameworks.
