# Preeti Kota

Head of Engineering, Compass, Atlassian

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

She's spent over 20 years in engineering and has experience building products from scratch, developing large-scale enterprise applications, and leading global engineering teams. She is a firm believer that people are at the center of everything Atlassian does and is excited to build Compass based on Atlassian Engineering practices and ways of working. Outside of work, Preeti is exploring karate with her daughter and enjoys playing board games with her family.
