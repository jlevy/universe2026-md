# Helen Lau

[Commonwealth Bank](../organizations/commonwealth-bank.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2025 as General Manager Engineering Platform, Commonwealth Bank of Australia.

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)
- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

Helen is the general manager for the Engineering Platform at the Commonwealth Bank of Australia, where she leads transformative engineering initiatives that empower thousands of developers across the organization. With over 20 years of experience in software engineering, architecture, technical consulting, and executive leadership, Helen is known for driving innovation at scale while fostering a culture of excellence and inclusion.

Helen is passionate about building modern engineering platforms that accelerate delivery, embed security by design, and enable teams to ship software with speed and confidence. Her work spans DevSecOps maturity, automated security programs, and platform engineering capabilities that support the bank’s digital ambitions.
She has presented at numerous international conferences, forums, and industry panels, and is deeply committed to being part of the global community leading engineering transformation. Helen thrives on exchanging ideas, learning from others, and contributing to the broader ecosystem, bringing CBA’s engineering journey to the international stage through thought leadership and collaboration.

Prior to joining CBA, Helen held leadership roles at Microsoft and Caltex/Ampol Australia, where she led cloud transformation, sustainability initiatives, and digital innovation hubs.
