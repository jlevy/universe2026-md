# John Ruiz

[GitHub](../organizations/github.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2023 as Staff Security Engineer, GitHub.

- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

John Ruiz is a Staff Security Engineer on the Secure Access Engineering team where he protects the infrastructure that runs GitHub and designs and develops the tools used by Hubbers to obtain secure access to the infrastructure, data, and 3rd-party tools used to make our products. Prior to his role at GitHub, John was the VP of Engineering at YouEye and Principal R&D Engineer at Berico Technologies. In his free time, he writes code to automate his home, solar panels, and garden. He lives near Penn State University with his partner and children.
