# Robert Daugherty

Chief Security Officer, Sierra Nevada Corporation (SNC)

- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

As Chief Security Officer for Sierra Nevada Corporation (SNC), Robert Daugherty leads a holistic Security Program that includes Classified Networks and Programs, Classified Facilities, Counterintelligence, Cybersecurity, Insider Risk, Personnel Security, Operational Security, Business Continuity, Crisis Management, and Safety. SNC’s Security Program is recognized by the USG as being one of the top performing in all of industry with multiple DCSA Cogswell Awards for program excellence. Armed with an MBA, BCompSc, and CISSP, CISM certifications, Robert’s extensive background includes 25+ years of moving organizations toward excellence by leveraging technology to improve mission in rapidly evolving high-growth startup business units, highly matrixed enterprises, critical infrastructure, and the Defense and Intelligence sectors. Robert’s security mantra is “assume breach”; organizations must adopt a proactive counterintelligence, data-driven and risk-based mindset, constantly hunting for and quickly detecting anomalous internal and external activity.
