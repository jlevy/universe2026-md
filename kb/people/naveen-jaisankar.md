# Naveen Jaisankar

[Cathay Pacific](../organizations/cathay-pacific.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2025 as DevSecOps Lead, Cathay Pacific Airlines.

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

As a product-minded DevSecOps leader with 14 years of engineering experience, Naveen is a security speed enabler. In his current post as DevSecOps practice lead at Cathay Pacific, he architects agent-driven guard-rails that weave automated risk checks and policy enforcement into every stage of the airline’s software-delivery pipeline.

Naveen’s insight is sought after on the conference circuit — most recently at AWS Summit Hong Kong 2025, where he unpacked Cathay’s journey from conventional DevOps to an agentic AI-powered DevSecOps model before a theatre of cloud practitioners. His credibility rests on a career that began with micro-services and platform automation and is anchored by an engineering degree from Government College of Technology, Coimbatore.

Beyond his day job, Naveen is a new dad. His son arrived on May 30, 2025, and he recharges by trail-hiking in Hong Kong — a chance to debug ideas away from the terminal.
