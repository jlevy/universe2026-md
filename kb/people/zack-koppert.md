# Zack Koppert

[GitHub](../organizations/github.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2023 as Senior Software Engineer, GitHub.

- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

Zack is a Senior Software Engineer at GitHub on the OSPO team, with a focus on open source and innersource software development. He has a passion for collaborative coding and solving complex technical problems with teams. Zack is also an active member of the InnerSource Commons community, where he shares his knowledge and experiences with other like-minded developers.

Previously, Zack was a Senior DevOps Engineer on the Professional Services Team at GitHub and helped customers adopt GitHub, then guide them through DevSecOps, innersource, and open source transformations. Before GitHub, he founded and led the Open Source Office at Tektronix, focusing on both open source and innersource.

Zack lives in Oregon with his wife, son, dog, and two cats, and enjoys dirt bike racing and guitars.
