# Lessley Dennington

Senior Solutions Engineer, GitHub

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Lessley is a solutions engineer at GitHub, where she partners with customers to solve complex technical challenges and improve developer productivity. She’s particularly passionate about helping teams build smarter through automation, AI, and DevEx best practices. Prior to her current role, she spent five years as a software engineer working on internal engineering systems at GitHub and Microsoft. 

Lessley holds a master’s degree in computer science from Vanderbilt University and a bachelor’s degree in management information systems from The University of Alabama. Outside of work, she enjoys managing her Airbnb properties, exploring the great outdoors with her dog, and concocting new, adventurous kitchen creations.
