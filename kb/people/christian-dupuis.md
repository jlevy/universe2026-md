# Christian Dupuis

Sr. Principal Software Engineer, Docker

- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

Christian is a Senior Principal Engineer at Docker and a member of the Docker Scout product development team, focusing on building products to help Docker’s users secure their software supply chain.

Christian spent more than two decades working in open source software as well as being involved in early stage startups; starting as an early member of the engineering team behind the Spring Framework, to co-founding Atomist, focused on software delivery and software supply chain aspects.

When not in front of a screen, Christian is a husband, father of two soon-to-be teenage daughters as well as an ambitious road bike rider.
