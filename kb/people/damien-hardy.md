# Damien Hardy

Senior Service Delivery Engineer, GitHub

- **GitHub Universe 2024:** speaker (confirmed)

Damien is a dedicated service delivery professional and solution architect specializing in helping organizations get the most out of their technology. At GitHub, he works closely with a wide range of clients, crafting solutions that boost platform performance and efficiency. Throughout his career, Damien has focused on delivering high-quality service and building strong, lasting partnerships.

Before joining GitHub, Damien sharpened his developer skills at PRA Group, where he was instrumental in modernizing development processes and streamlining DevOps practices. His efforts there led to more efficient workflows and more robust, maintainable code. He also implemented knowledge-sharing initiatives and worked to bridge the European and American developer communities. Earlier, at Allocate Software, Damien led the creation of bespoke applications for clients around the world, consistently going above and beyond to meet their needs.

What drives Damien is a passion for solving complex problems and a commitment to delivering results that truly make a difference. When he's not working, he enjoys the classic pleasures of food and travel, with Karachi being his favorite destination for both.
