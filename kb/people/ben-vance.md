[GitHub](../organizations/github.md)

Listed for GitHub Universe 2023 as Senior Support Engineer, GitHub.

- **GitHub Universe 2023:** speaker (confirmed)

Ben Vance is a seasoned support professional with over a decade of experience in technical customer service. As a Senior Support Engineer at GitHub, he brings a wealth of expertise in the critical domains of account security and authentication. 

Ben's journey at GitHub began in 2015, and since then, he has played a pivotal role in safeguarding the digital identities of users across the platform. Prior to GitHub, Ben distinguished himself as a Support Operations Manager and a Certified Scrum Master (CSM) at Campaign Monitor. With an extensive background in customer support and a specialization in account security, Ben is uniquely positioned to share invaluable insights into the evolving landscape of digital protection. 

Beyond the tech realm, Ben finds solace in the great outdoors as an avid backpacker and camper. However, his most cherished role is that of a dedicated dad to his twin boys.
