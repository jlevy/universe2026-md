# Daniel Connolly

Strategic, GitHub

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Daniel works with some of the largest and most strategic FSI customers in the world on their digital and AI transformations with GitHub. He's been with GitHub for six years in this capacity and has contributed to standing up the FSI vertical while working with firms such as Liberty Mutual, Fidelity Investments, Citigroup, Goldman Sachs, and others. Fueled by people, curiosity, and a regular dose of improv, he was born and raised in Boston—and he does a wicked terrible job of hiding it.
