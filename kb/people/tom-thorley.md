# Tom Thorley

Senior Director Safety & Integrity, GitHub

- **GitHub Universe 2025:** speaker (confirmed)

Tom is a senior director of safety and integrity at GitHub. His responsibilities include ensuring user safety, countering harassment, malware detection, content moderation, and eradicating CSAM and TVEC on the platform.

Prior to GitHub, Tom worked for over a decade at the British government’s signals intelligence agency, GCHQ, where he specialized in issues at the nexus of technology and human behavior. There, he built and operationalized data science teams to inform operations and discover counterterrorism threats. 

Tom then spent five years working with U.S. government, military, and intelligence agencies to coordinate intergovernmental relationships between the U.S. and U.K., and to provide expert consultancy on cyber issues, disinformation, technology strategy, and operational planning. After leaving government service, Tom became the first Director of Technology, Engineering, and Solutions at GIFCT, where he worked to prevent terrorists and violent extremists from exploiting digital platforms—building GIFCT's hash-sharing database and incident response framework.

As a passionate advocate for responsible technology, Tom is a founding member of the board for the LA and Southern California Internet Society Chapter, where he focuses on building an open and secure internet for all and a member of the AI Council for America On Tech. He also allocates time for volunteerism with DataKind and All Tech Is Human.
