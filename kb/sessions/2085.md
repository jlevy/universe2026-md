# Volunteer with GitHub Gives

**Thu Oct 29 2026, 8:00 AM–9:00 AM PDT** · [Equator Coffee](../rooms/equator-coffee-fort-mason.yaml)

Start day two of Universe by giving back to our host city. Join GitHub Gives and local nonprofit [Refuse Refuse](https://refuserefusesf.org/) for a community cleanup around Fort Mason Center. Meet directly across the road from Equator Coffee at 8:00 a.m. PT for an introduction from Refuse Refuse before the group heads out for the cleanup. Please wear closed-toe shoes, dress for the weather, and bring a jacket - the San Francisco waterfront can be chilly. Connect with fellow attendees while taking action together to help keep San Francisco clean and beautiful.

- **Type:** Social
