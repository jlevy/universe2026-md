# Building the vehicle of the future on GitHub with Arm

**Thu Nov 9 2023, 11:15 AM–11:45 AM PST** · [Main Stage](../../rooms/ybca-main-stage.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Christopher Rumpf](../../people/christopher-rumpf.md)

The Software Defined Vehicle is creating a once-in-a-lifetime disruption in today’s automotive industry, with each car projected to be driving around with over one billion lines of code working together. Car manufacturers and their suppliers are moving from discrete components that plug and play to rich, complex components with APIs. 

In this talk, Christopher Rumpf, Senior Director of Arm’s Automotive business, will show you how GitHub’s developer, enterprise security, and AI services are perfectly suited for embedded software teams that leverage the Arm instruction set. 

Rumpf will give you a tour of how the automotive supply chain is collaborating safely and securely with modern, efficient standardized tools and much more flexible methodologies, which are allowing the automotive industry to innovate faster than ever before.

- **Type:** Main Stage
- **Track:** Developer Experience
- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
- **Topics:** Continuous Integration + Continuous Deployment (CI/CD)
- **GitHub products:** Actions
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developers, Open Source Developers or Maintainers, Startups
