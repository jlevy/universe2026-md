# Driving organizational sustainability with platform engineering

**Wed Nov 8 2023, 10:30 AM–11:10 AM PST** · [Theater](../../rooms/sfmoma-theater.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Lesley Cordero](../../people/lesley-cordero.md)

Organizations face consequences when prioritizing short-term gains over long-term ones, leading to tech debt, retention issues, and business risk. Platform engineering drives organizational sustainability through DevOps principles, support systems, and a standardized architecture.

In this session, we'll cover: 
1) DevOps sociotechnical principles
2) Community-driven support systems
3) Platform architecture, its design tensions, and a framework for building robust developer experiences

We'll dive into each of these components through the lens of the feature development cycle.

- **Type:** General Session
- **Track:** Developer Experience
- **Level:** Level 200: Intermediate
- **Topics:** Continuous Integration + Continuous Deployment (CI/CD), Git and related code management techniques, Open Source, Software Engineering, Testing and related practices, Community
- **GitHub products:** Actions
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developers
