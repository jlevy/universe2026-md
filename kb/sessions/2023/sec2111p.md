# The story of how we enforced 2FA for millions of users

**Thu Nov 9 2023, 12:30 PM–1:10 PM PST** · [Screening Room](../../rooms/ybca-screening-room.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Hirsch Singhal](../../people/hirsch-singhal.md) (panelist), [Ben Vance](../../people/ben-vance.md) (panelist), [John D. Swanson](../../people/john-d-swanson.md) (moderator), [Hannah Gould](../../people/hannah-gould.md) (panelist)

At GitHub, we believe that our unique position as the home for all developers means that we have both an opportunity and a responsibility to raise the bar for security across the software development ecosystem. So, in 2023, we began requiring millions of users on GitHub.com to enable 2FA. In this panel discussion, we’ll take you on the multi-year journey to dramatically increase our already high 2FA coverage without hurting the user experience, overwhelming our support team, or reducing security. You’ll learn about how we approached the project and challenges we encountered from the perspectives of security, support, and product management professionals, while gleaning key insights into the approaches other platforms can use to enhance their community's security through increased 2FA adoption.

- **Topics:** Open Source, Security, Supply Chain Security, Threat Modeling
- **GitHub products:** Supply Chain Security, Enterprise/Admin
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Open Source Developers or Maintainers, Security Professionals, Security Leadership
