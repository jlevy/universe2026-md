# How to innovate faster by standardizing delivery and automating governance

**Wed Nov 8 2023, 3:30 PM–4:10 PM PST** · [Theater](../../rooms/sfmoma-theater.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Caleb Queern](../../people/caleb-queern.md), [James Williams](../../people/james-williams.md)

Using a single platform for development, security, and operations is a powerful strategy for improving efficiency, communication, and security in the SDLC. Once you're on the journey of a standardized experience, automating governance reduces the subjectivity in decision-making. 

In this session, you'll learn how to say goodbye to painful or futile change review processes. Your change approvers will thank you for sparing their time and granting them the tools to better understand whether code changes satisfy the policy checks that matter for your project.

- **Type:** General Session
- **Track:** Developer Experience, Security
- **Level:** Level 200: Intermediate
- **Topics:** DevOps, DevSecOps, Monitoring and Observability, Security, Testing and related practices, Threat Modeling, Vulnerability Detection
- **GitHub products:** Actions, Advanced Security, Supply Chain Security, Enterprise/Admin
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developers, Security Professionals, Security Leadership
