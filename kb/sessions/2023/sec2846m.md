# Unlocking accelerated innovation without compromising customer trust

**Thu Nov 9 2023, 1:45 PM–2:25 PM PST** · [Main Stage](../../rooms/ybca-main-stage.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Vincent King](../../people/vincent-king.md) (panelist), [Mike Hanley](../../people/mike-hanley.md) (moderator), [Sigurd Pollmann](../../people/sigurd-pollmann.md) (panelist), [Helen Lau](../../people/helen-lau.md) (panelist), [Gerard McMahon](../../people/gerard-mcmahon.md) (panelist)

Software development is a dynamic industry, and achieving accelerated growth relies on elevating the developer experience to unlock innovation, while steadfastly upholding customer trust. These seemingly opposing concepts beg the question: Can you truly attain business agility, developer contentment, enhanced productivity, seamless collaboration, and unwavering security all at once? Join us in this session for insights from GitHub's Chief Security Officer and SVP of Engineering, Michael Hanley, and experts in heavily regulated sectors—like banking and finance—from Fidelity, CBA, Deutsche Börse Group, and Bank of England as they share their proven strategies for effectively building, scaling, and fortifying software in today's swiftly evolving digital landscape.

- **Track:** Security
- **Level:** Level 200: Intermediate
- **Topics:** Security, Threat Modeling, Vulnerability Detection, Collaboration, Productivity
- **GitHub products:** Advanced Security
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developers, Security Professionals, Security Leadership
- **Mentions:** [Deutsche Börse](../../organizations/deutsche-borse.md), [Bank of England](../../organizations/bank-of-england.md)
