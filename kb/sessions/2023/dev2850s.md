# Democratizing the creation of AI / ML apps, how to put this tech into every engineers hands

**Thu Nov 9 2023, 10:55 AM–11:15 AM PST** · [Host Studio](../../rooms/ybca-host-studio.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Scott Johnston](../../people/scott-johnston.md)

Ten years ago Docker emerged as a linchpin technology in the world of cloud-native development, profoundly impacting both the cloud-native and developer communities. In today’s rapidly evolving landscape, where AI/ML are transforming industries, one pressing question arises: How do we make these game-changing technologies accessible to every developer? Today we are joined by Docker CEO Scott Johnston who is here to shed light on the concept of democratizing AI/ML app creation, exploring the strategies and approaches that empower engineers from diverse backgrounds to tap into the potential of AI/ML for the next generation of software development.

- **Track:** AI, Developer Experience
- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
- **Topics:** Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Developers, Open Source Developers or Maintainers
- **Mentions:** [Docker](../../software/docker.yaml)
