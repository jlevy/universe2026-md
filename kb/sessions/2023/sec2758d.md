# GitHub Advanced Security: Secret Scanning

**Thu Nov 9 2023, 11:00 AM–11:25 AM PST** · [Demo Stage](../../rooms/ybca-demo-stage.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Zain Malik](../../people/zain-malik.md), [Courtney Claessens](../../people/courtney-claessens.md), [Daniel Shanahan](../../people/daniel-shanahan.md)

Stop secret leaks with GitHub Advanced Security’s Secret Scanning. In this demo, you’ll learn how to keep secrets out of the public eye, no matter how the secret is structured. It scans for over 200 token types, and is supported by a partner program of more than 100 service providers to detect leaked secrets at scale. You’ll experience the power of AI in detecting generic secrets, like passwords, and writing custom patterns to ensure that all of your organization’s secrets stay just that: secret.
