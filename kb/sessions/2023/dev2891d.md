# Platform engineering made easy: Octopus Deploy’s answer to DevEx

**Thu Nov 9 2023, 1:30 PM–1:55 PM PST** · [Demo Stage](../../rooms/ybca-demo-stage.yaml)

**Speakers:** [James Chatmas](../../people/james-chatmas.md)

In the ever-evolving world of software development, developer experience (DevEx) often gets interrupted by tedious and time-consuming tasks. This presentation demonstrates how Octopus Deploy improves DevEx by automating the entire lifecycle of onboarding a new application—from GitHub repository creation to deployment and smoke testing—in a matter of minutes. 

Discover how platform engineering with Octopus Deploy can eliminate bottlenecks and accelerate your DevOps processes, letting developers focus on what matters to them the most: writing quality code.
