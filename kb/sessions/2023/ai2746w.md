# Workshop: How GitHub Copilot improves development practices

**Thu Nov 9 2023, 10:30 AM–12:00 PM PST** · [Hyatt - Gallery Ballroom II](../../rooms/hyatt-gallery-ballroom-ii.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Dhruv Gupta](../../people/dhruv-gupta.md) (facilitator), [Josh Feng](../../people/josh-feng.md) (facilitator)

**Session must be added during registration in order to add to your schedule** 

In this session, we delve into a real-world feature development scenario, constructing a functional feature from a baseline project. We'll integrate AI capabilities of GitHub Copilot at various development stages—shedding light on the efficacy and relevance of the prompts provided. 
 
The exploration extends to the novel features being explored through GitHub Copilot that aim to streamline the development process, enhancing the ease of integration and accelerating the overall development timeline. 
 
Through this practical application, we'll present a hands-on experience with AI in development and share significant insights on the potential role AI can play in boosting developer productivity and simplifying coding practices.

- **Level:** Level 200: Intermediate
- **Topics:** Artificial Intelligence (AI), Continuous Integration + Continuous Deployment (CI/CD), DevOps, Software Engineering, Productivity
- **GitHub products:** Copilot
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Developers
