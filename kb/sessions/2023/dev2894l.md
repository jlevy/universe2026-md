# Is complete end-to-end observability even possible?

**Wed Nov 8 2023, 1:30 PM–2:10 PM PST** · [Discussions Lounge](../../rooms/ybca-discussions-lounge.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Ben Michel](../../people/ben-michel.md) (facilitator)

As modern systems increase in complexity and technical organizations become fractured into silos—with QA teams, DevOps teams, SRE teams, and more—the number of tools needed to ensure that our applications work as expected has also exploded. When a deployment takes a painfully long time or a bug slips into production, finding and resolving the issue often descends into chaos and blame.

In this session, we’ll share tips for achieving complete end-to-end observability for your entire technical organization in a single place. You’ll have an opportunity to discuss your challenges and share your experiences with other attendees, and together we’ll identify actionable steps to improve your visibility in all aspects of the software development lifecycle.

- **Type:** Discussion Lounge
- **Track:** Developer Experience
- **Level:** Level 200: Intermediate
- **Topics:** Monitoring and Observability
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developers, Open Source Developers or Maintainers, Security Professionals, Security Leadership
