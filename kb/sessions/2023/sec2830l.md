# Discussing the transparency and accuracy of vulnerability information

**Thu Nov 9 2023, 2:30 PM–3:10 PM PST** · [Discussions Lounge](../../rooms/ybca-discussions-lounge.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Xavier René-Corail](../../people/xavier-rene-corail.md) (facilitator), [Madison Ficorilli](../../people/madison-ficorilli.md) (facilitator), [Kean Hankins](../../people/kean-hankins.md) (facilitator)

As software continues to drive the global digital economy, Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures (CVE) remain paramount in strengthening software supply chain security. We'll focus on the CVE journey from its initial discovery, to its inclusion in databases like the GitHub Advisory Database, and our security research and advisory database curation experts. Together we'll delve into the processes and criteria used to assess severity, the factors that contribute to this, and explore how the various stakeholders involved in CVE management influence the transparency and accuracy of CVE information. We’ll also explore what happens when a CVE is challenged, how the community can weigh in, and the potential impact for users and organizations. Join us to share your perspective, ask your questions, and learn from your peers on how we can all work together to improve the CVE process.

- **Type:** Discussion Lounge
- **Track:** Security
- **Level:** Level 200: Intermediate
- **Topics:** Security, Supply Chain Security, Vulnerability Detection, Community
- **GitHub products:** Advanced Security, Supply Chain Security
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developers, Open Source Developers or Maintainers, Security Professionals, Security Leadership
