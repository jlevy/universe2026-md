# How to do what AI just CAN'T: Proving your value when code is commoditized

**Thu Nov 9 2023, 1:30 PM–2:10 PM PST** · [Discussions Lounge](../../rooms/ybca-discussions-lounge.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Brook Riggio](../../people/brook-riggio.md) (facilitator)

Suddenly faced with very capable AI developer tools—such as LLM-based code completion and chatbots that can write and analyze code—devs need to adjust. Being able to whip up an app over a weekend is no longer impressive when an AI tool can do it in a matter of minutes. So how can developers evolve? We need to get good at what AI can't handle. 

In this session, we will look at how being human is a huge asset on dev teams. We'll present strategies for thriving in an AI-driven world, and communicating your irreplaceable value to your boss or hiring manager.
