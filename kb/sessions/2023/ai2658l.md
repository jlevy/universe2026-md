# Unlocking the power of AI in open source, the ethical way

**Wed Nov 8 2023, 12:30 PM–1:10 PM PST** · [Discussions Lounge](../../rooms/ybca-discussions-lounge.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Quiana Berry](../../people/quiana-berry.md) (facilitator)

In an era where AI tools like ChatGPT and GitHub Copilot are integral to software development, it is crucial to address their impact on diverse communities. This session explores the implications of algorithmic biases in AI-driven technologies and provides insights on how to responsibly leverage their power to foster diversity and inclusion within the GitHub community. By establishing ethical frameworks, and tracking and mitigating biases at their source, we can unlock the full potential of open source and GitHub, while ensuring the well-being of diverse users and communities worldwide.
