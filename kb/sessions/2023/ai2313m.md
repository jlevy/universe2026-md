# Getting to yes: What you need to greenlight AI tools at your company

**Thu Nov 9 2023, 12:45 PM–1:25 PM PST** · [Main Stage](../../rooms/ybca-main-stage.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Shelley McKinley](../../people/shelley-mckinley.md) (moderator), [Heather Meeker](../../people/heather-meeker.md) (panelist), [Jen Peck](../../people/jen-peck.md) (panelist)

When developers bring AI tools and similar innovations to their company for adoption, legal review and new policies are often required before wide implementation. We saw it with open source, and we’re seeing it now with AI. 
 
Developers can remove barriers to adoption by helping decision-makers understand the business benefits of AI tools and how to navigate the legal issues that arise when using them. In this session, we’ll explore how legal experts and open source and enterprise software leaders are implementing policies and practices that manage risk and enable responsible innovation.
