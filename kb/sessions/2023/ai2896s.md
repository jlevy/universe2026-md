# How to boost Generative AI’s value in software engineering

**Thu Nov 9 2023, 2:25 PM–2:45 PM PST** · [Host Studio](../../rooms/ybca-host-studio.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Teresa Tung](../../people/teresa-tung.md)

From gathering requirements through to designing, building, testing, deploying and operating applications, Gen AI offers the prospect of significant gains in productivity, quality, modernization speed and talent retention across the software development lifecycle. 
 
Getting value from Gen AI in software engineering is a profound and complex journey, far bigger than a standard technology implementation. Accenture’s Teresa Tung will discuss how to best realize value leveraging the right talent, workflows, enterprise readiness, the use of technology ecosystems, and responsible AI.
