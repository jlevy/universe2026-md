# Using AI for Good: Supporting global health progress

**Thu Nov 9 2023, 3:30 PM–3:55 PM PST** · [Demo Stage](../../rooms/ybca-demo-stage.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Miquel Duran-Frigola](../../people/miquel-duran-frigola.md) (panelist), [Sid Espinosa](../../people/sid-espinosa.md) (moderator)

Generative AI is changing our world. We know that AI increases developer productivity and lowers barriers of entry into tech, but did you know it is also applied to biomedical research in the global south? Have you considered the use of AI to help developers make tooling more accessible? Listen up as Miquel Duran-Frigola, Lead Scientist & Co-Founder of the Ersilia Open Source Initiative, shares how AI transformed their ways of working, helping accelerate human progress to those who need it the most.

- **Type:** Fireside Chat
- **Track:** AI
- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
- **Topics:** Accessibility, Artificial Intelligence (AI), DevOps, Open Source, Social Impact, Software Engineering, Collaboration, Community, Productivity
- **GitHub products:** Copilot, Enterprise/Admin, Projects, Issues
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developers, Open Source Developers or Maintainers, Startups, Security Leadership, Educators
- **Mentions:** [Ersilia Open Source Initiative](../../organizations/ersilia-open-source-initiative.md)
