# Generative AI and the future of development

**Thu Nov 9 2023, 2:00 PM–2:25 PM PST** · [Demo Stage](../../rooms/ybca-demo-stage.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Christopher Harrison](../../people/christopher-harrison.md), [Allison Weins](../../people/allison-weins.md)

Since someone fed the first stack of punch cards into a computer to provide it a set of instructions, we've always been looking for ways to streamline development. We've got new languages, frameworks, tools—some of which don't even require code! Through it all, the demand for apps and features has yet to be satiated, and the need for developers hasn't waned. Developers have learned new skills and adopted new techniques to get the most out of what's available to them.

Generative AI and GitHub Copilot provide yet another tool, opening up a new set of capabilities and challenges. What can GitHub Copilot do? What can't it do? How can we get the most out of it? And will it replace my job? We'll answer all of these questions—and more—by providing practical guidance to help you realize GitHub Copilot's full potential in helping you be a better developer.
