# Platform engineering: a new idea or just a new name?

**Wed Nov 8 2023, 11:30 AM–12:10 PM PST** · [Discussions Lounge](../../rooms/ybca-discussions-lounge.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Mike Nguyen](../../people/mike-nguyen.md) (facilitator), [Michael Richardson](../../people/michael-richardson.md) (facilitator)

As the enthusiasm and demand for platform engineering continue to grow, teams responsible for creating an Internal Developer Platform must work out how to get a strong start and regularly demonstrate value to ensure long-term funding continues. 

In this session, you'll learn about the patterns and anti-patterns that have emerged as people adopt platform engineering, along with the ideal conditions for an IDP that are most likely to result in a successful outcome. You'll also discover ways to measure the impact of an IDP to guide its development and demonstrate its value to stakeholders.

- **Type:** Discussion Lounge
- **Track:** Developer Experience
- **Level:** Level 200: Intermediate
- **Topics:** Software Engineering, Collaboration, Productivity
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developers, Open Source Developers or Maintainers, Educators
