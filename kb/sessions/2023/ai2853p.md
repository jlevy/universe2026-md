# From fiction to function: How AI agents will reshape developer workflows

**Thu Nov 9 2023, 3:30 PM–4:10 PM PST** · [Screening Room](../../rooms/ybca-screening-room.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Andrew Rice](../../people/andrew-rice.md) (moderator), [Eddie Aftandilian](../../people/eddie-aftandilian.md) (panelist), [Eirini Kalliamvakou](../../people/eirini-kalliamvakou.md) (panelist), [Rahul Pandita](../../people/rahul-pandita.md) (panelist)

Remember when AI agents were the stuff of science fiction, like Hal 9000 and Jarvis? Today, AI agents are becoming mainstream (okay, minus the Hollywood dazzle), promising to help developers in virtually every task. As tool builders, we ask the big question: How do we integrate AI into workflows to elevate developers’ work, identity, and creative expression? 

Join us for a fireside chat with experts from GitHub Next, and hear about their research and explorations to thoughtfully augment how developers work by leveraging AI agents.

- **Type:** Panel Discussion
- **Track:** AI
- **Level:** Level 200: Intermediate
- **Topics:** Artificial Intelligence (AI), Human Factors, Machine Learning, Software Engineering, Productivity
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Developers, Open Source Developers or Maintainers
