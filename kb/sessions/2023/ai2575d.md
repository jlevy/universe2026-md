# How intentional prompts create game-changing suggestions

**Wed Nov 8 2023, 2:00 PM–2:25 PM PST** · [Demo Stage](../../rooms/ybca-demo-stage.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Rizel Scarlett](../../people/rizel-scarlett.md)

Generative AI coding assistants are revolutionizing the way we code, but it can feel frustrating when these tools produce irrelevant or absent suggestions. Just like with any emerging tech, there's a learning curve to using the new tools. Join GitHub Copilot–enthusiast Rizel Scarlett as she shares proven strategies and expert tips to transform your random prompts into intentional ones, unlocking a world of game-changing suggestions. Get inspired, get creative, and get ready to embrace the future of coding!
