# The playbook: successfully launch your project in open source

**Thu Nov 9 2023, 12:30 PM–1:10 PM PST** · [Discussions Lounge](../../rooms/ybca-discussions-lounge.yaml)

**Speakers:** [JZ Zhang](../../people/jz-zhang.md) (facilitator)

Does the idea of marketing your open source project make you cringe? Making your project stand out requires more than just a good product and good code. Join our discussion with JZ Zhang, COO of Charm, an open source company making the command line creative, to chat about best practices for authentic marketing in open source! 

During the session, you'll learn how to identify your target audience, build and nurture a community of contributors, engage users, strategically promote to various channels, and measure your success even after launching.

- **Type:** Discussion Lounge
- **Track:** Developer Experience
- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
- **Topics:** Open Source, Community, Productivity
- **GitHub products:** Projects
- **Audience:** Open Source Developers or Maintainers, Startups, Educators
