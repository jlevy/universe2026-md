# Pipeline observability: The key to better software team performance

**Thu Nov 9 2023, 2:30 PM–2:55 PM PST** · [Demo Stage](../../rooms/ybca-demo-stage.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Kassen Qian](../../people/kassen-qian.md)

According to the DevOps Research and Assessment (DORA) team, software team performance can be directly correlated to deployment frequency, lead time for changes, change failure rate, and time to restore services after failures—and your CI/CD workflows are at the heart of all of these! Pipeline observability is the key to ensuring that your deployments are fast and that bugs are caught before they reach production.

In this session, we’ll show you how Datadog can help you find issues in your CI/CD workflows by uncovering bottlenecks and surfacing flaky tests that add friction to your deployments. You’ll learn how to get better visibility, accelerate software delivery, and ultimately improve your software team's performance.

- **Type:** Product Demo
- **Track:** Developer Experience
- **Level:** Level 200: Intermediate
- **Topics:** Continuous Integration + Continuous Deployment (CI/CD), DevOps
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developers, Open Source Developers or Maintainers, Security Professionals, Security Leadership
- **Mentions:** [Datadog](../../organizations/datadog.md)
