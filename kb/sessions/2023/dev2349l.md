# We eat our own: rethinking open source

**Wed Nov 8 2023, 3:30 PM–4:10 PM PST** · [Discussions Lounge](../../rooms/ybca-discussions-lounge.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Justin Schroeder](../../people/justin-schroeder.md) (facilitator)

We all love open source. Free software? Yes, please! But the recent conversation around "developer experience" should cover open source creators, too—because the truth is that open source maintainers need better support.

In this discussion, we’ll look for solutions to common open source problems such as lack of funding, user entitlement, competition, and maintainer burnout. Join us as we brainstorm ways to build a healthier open source community.

- **Track:** Developer Experience
- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
- **Topics:** Human Factors, Open Source, Social Impact, Software Engineering, Collaboration, Community, Productivity
- **GitHub products:** Discussions, Projects, Issues, Sponsors
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developers, Open Source Developers or Maintainers
