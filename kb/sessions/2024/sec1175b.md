# Mission Copilot Autofix: Securing the world's software

**Tue Oct 29 2024, 3:30 PM–4:10 PM PDT** · [Cowell Theater](../../rooms/cowell-theater-gateway-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Niroshan Rajadurai](../../people/niroshan-rajadurai.md), [Nick Liffen](../../people/nick-liffen.md)

AI is far from reaching its peak in application security. Join Nick Liffen, director of GitHub Advanced Security, and Niroshan Rajadurai, VP, GTM, at GitHub, as they share how GitHub applies AI to help streamline remediation and enhance alert interpretability. This session will delve into real-world applications and a future where AI fortifies defenses and helps preemptively identify and mitigate emerging threats. This session is designed for developers, security experts, and executives alike. Come to learn and stay for the exciting conclusion. Our speakers have a special surprise planned!
