# What you CVE isn't always what you get: Conceptual severity vs. real risk

**Wed Oct 30 2024, 3:30 PM–4:10 PM PDT** · [Discussions Lounge](../../rooms/discussions-lounge-firehouse.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Kevin Backhouse](../../people/kevin-backhouse.md) (facilitator), [Jon Moroney](../../people/jon-moroney.md) (facilitator)

Tens of thousands of Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures (CVEs) are published each year, and every year the number rises. Yet how many vulnerabilities are actually exploitable and require prioritization? 

Not all CVEs hold equal weight. Some make the news while others fly under the radar. In this discussion facilitated by GitHub's Jon Moroney, security analyst III, and Kevin Backhouse, staff manager of Security Research, you'll take a look at a selection of interesting CVEs, break into groups, and choose one to analyze in more depth. You'll consider and discuss: Is the CVE exploitable? Will it be used for large-scale campaigns or more targeted attacks? We will discuss what’s hype and what’s not and share ideas to improve the process.

- **Type:** Discussion Lounge
- **Track:** Security
- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
- **Topics:** Open Source, Security, Supply Chain Security, Collaboration, Community
- **GitHub products:** Advanced Security, Supply Chain Security
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developers, Open Source Developers or Maintainers, Security Professionals, Security Leadership
