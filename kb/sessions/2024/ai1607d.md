# Optimizing your dev flow with GitHub Copilot

**Wed Oct 30 2024, 11:30 AM–11:55 AM PDT** · [Demo Stage](../../rooms/demo-stage-gateway-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Christopher Harrison](../../people/christopher-harrison.md), [Kedasha Kerr](../../people/kedasha-kerr.md)

New tools always come with a learning curve, and GitHub Copilot is no exception. Just like working with a new colleague, collaborating with AI takes practice, experimentation, and collaboration.

GitHub Developer Advocates Kedasha Kerr and Christopher Harrison will share their insights on how to get the most out of GitHub Copilot. They’ll walk through real-world examples highlighting the many ways you’ll interact with your AI-pair programmer. You’ll leave with several useful tips you can apply to your flow, helping you get guidance on tasks of all sizes.
