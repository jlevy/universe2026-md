# Collaborative API creation, testing, and deployment with Postman

**Tue Oct 29 2024, 2:30 PM–2:55 PM PDT** · [Demo Stage](../../rooms/demo-stage-gateway-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Alex Barnett](../../people/alex-barnett.md)

Join Alex Barnett, head of DevRel & Community at Postman, to learn how Postman's collaboration-centric approach transforms the developer experience and makes it easier for teams to build, test, and deploy high-quality APIs.

- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
