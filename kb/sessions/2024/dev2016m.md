# #HappyCoding! Build intelligent apps with Visual Studio Code, GitHub, and Azure

**Tue Oct 29 2024, 2:45 PM–3:25 PM PDT** · [Main Stage](../../rooms/main-stage-festival-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Amanda Silver](../../people/amanda-silver.md), [Shayne Boyer](../../people/shayne-boyer.md), [Soojin (Min) Choi](../../people/soojin-min-choi.md), [Burke Holland](../../people/burke-holland.md)

Join Microsoft's Amanda Silver, CVP for Microsoft’s Developer Division, Shayne Boyer, principal manager of Product Management, and Soojin Choi, senior product manager, for this exciting, demo-driven session. They'll showcase how you can get the most out of VS Code, GitHub, and Azure to rapidly build at-scale, intelligent applications. Whether you're a seasoned developer or just beginning your coding journey, this session will equip you with the knowledge and tools to harness the full potential of AI in your projects. #HappyCoding!

- **Level:** Level 200: Intermediate
- **Topics:** AI, Cloud, Continuous Integration + Continuous Deployment (CI/CD), Software Engineering, Productivity
- **GitHub products:** Copilot
