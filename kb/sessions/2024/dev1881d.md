# Figma for developers: Designed to ship great products, faster

**Tue Oct 29 2024, 11:00 AM–11:25 AM PDT** · [Demo Stage](../../rooms/demo-stage-gateway-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Jake Albaugh](../../people/jake-albaugh.md)

**Hosted by:** [Figma](../../organizations/figma.md)

Effective collaboration between developers and designers is critical for product development—and the right tools can help. Developers can ship quality code faster when they don't have to switch contexts to stay in sync with designers. Clear communication and the shared language of design systems allow teams to streamline the hand-off process and build more efficiently at scale. 

Join Jake Albaugh, developer advocate at Figma, to discover new features that help developers optimize how they work across Figma's Dev Mode and their codebases, and bring design and development closer together.

- **Type:** Product Demo
- **Track:** Developer Experience
- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
- **Topics:** Software Engineering, Collaboration, Productivity
- **GitHub products:** Actions, Extensibility/Ecosystem, Projects and Issues
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developers, Startups
