# Cha-ching! How to attract funding for your open source projects

**Tue Oct 29 2024, 10:30 AM–11:10 AM PDT** · [Sandbox Stage](../../rooms/sandbox-stage-general-s-residence.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Anjuan Simmons](../../people/anjuan-simmons.md) (facilitator)

There are many amazing stories about open source projects that have successfully attracted funding. Those dollars can translate to hiring more developers, speeding the release timeline, and tightening security. 

In this session, Anjuan Simmons, staff engineering manager at GitHub, will walk through real-life use cases, challenges faced and overcome, and outline key elements of successful funding profiles. Anjuan and members of GitHub's Open Source Funding team will also review attendee funding profiles live and provide real-time feedback and actionable changes that you can immediately make to help improve your profile and attract funding to your projects.

Note: This stage has limited capacity and will seat attendees at a first come first serve basis.  Adding this session to your schedule does not reserve a seat.  Please attend early.

- **Type:** Sandbox Session
- **Track:** Developer Experience
- **Level:** Level 300: Advanced
- **Topics:** Human Factors, Open Source, Collaboration, Community, Productivity
- **GitHub products:** Sponsors
- **Audience:** Open Source Developers or Maintainers
