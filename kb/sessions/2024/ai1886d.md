# It's a no brainer! How using AI for app requirements leads to better development outcomes

**Wed Oct 30 2024, 12:00 PM–12:25 PM PDT** · [Demo Stage](../../rooms/demo-stage-gateway-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Cory Hymel](../../people/cory-hymel.md)

Did you know that 70% of development projects fail due to poor requirements? Crowdbotics helps ensure projects succeed—and speeds them up by taking a strategic approach to AI. 

In this session with Cory Hymel, VP of Innovation and Research at Crowdbotics, you’ll see how the platform uses AI to dramatically improve the creation of product requirements and enterprise context and leverage that knowledge to accelerate the rest of the software development lifecycle. You'll also learn how the platform integrates with GitHub Copilot to improve outcomes and help your team innovate with unprecedented speed and efficiency, from requirements to deployment.

- **Type:** Product Demo
- **Track:** AI, Developer Experience
- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
- **Topics:** Agile practices, AI, Automated Infrastructure Deployment, Cloud, Continuous Integration + Continuous Deployment (CI/CD), DevOps, DevSecOps, Infrastructure as Code, Software Engineering, Productivity
- **GitHub products:** Actions, Copilot
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developers
- **Mentions:** [Crowdbotics](../../organizations/crowdbotics.md), [GitHub Copilot](../../software/github-copilot.yaml)
