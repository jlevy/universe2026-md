# Create type-safe SDKs for REST APIs in 10 minutes or less

**Wed Oct 30 2024, 3:00 PM–3:25 PM PDT** · [Demo Studio](../../rooms/demo-studio-gateway-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Sagar Batchu](../../people/sagar-batchu.md)

It’s no coincidence that the most successful API companies all support a wide range of SDKs for their users. SDKs give developers the ability to integrate with APIs in a dev-friendly manner, using their favorite languages and frameworks. That leads to faster integration and wider adoption of the API. It’s a win-win. Join Sagar Batchu, CEO and co-founder at Speakeasy, to explore how your organization can turbocharge its API adoption and how you can give your APIs the SDK-first treatment with automated GitHub workflows.
