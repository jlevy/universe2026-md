# Don't observe, debug: Discover how to quickly trace issues

**Tue Oct 29 2024, 11:30 AM–11:55 AM PDT** · [Demo Studio](../../rooms/demo-studio-gateway-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Sarah Guthals](../../people/sarah-guthals.md), [Cathy Teng](../../people/cathy-teng.md)

**Hosted by:** [Sentry](../../organizations/sentry.md)

"Something is broken." Hopefully, these are words you never hear from your end users. Even worse?  Hearing from your internal team that it's unclear what's wrong or that they're not sure how to fix a reported issue. 

Join this product demo session with Cathy Teng, software engineer at Sentry, and Sarah Guthals, director of developer relations at Sentry, to ensure you're receiving user feedback, sending alerts, and prioritizing user feedback. Cathy and Sarah will show you how to focus dev cycles on shipping new features, spend less time digging through logs, and how Sentry leverages GitHub Copilot to generate code fixes.
