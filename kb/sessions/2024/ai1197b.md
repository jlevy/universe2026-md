# Successfully scaling GitHub Copilot to thousands of developers

**Wed Oct 30 2024, 3:30 PM–4:10 PM PDT** · [Gallery 308](../../rooms/gallery-308-building-a.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Rob Bos](../../people/rob-bos.md)

There's a right and wrong way to roll out new technology. And spoiler alert: simply handing out licenses is the wrong way!

In this session, Rob Bos, DevOps consultant and GitHub trainer at Xebia, will share his experience training thousands of engineers to effectively use GitHub Copilot. He'll walk through what a comprehensive rollout and enablement plan looks like and share how to help engineers scale any learning curves. Rob will also share lessons learned along the way to supercharge usage and impact.
