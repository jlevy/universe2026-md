# Thomson Reuters' journey with GitHub Copilot—from assessment to adoption

**Tue Oct 29 2024, 3:30 PM–4:10 PM PDT** · [Gallery 308](../../rooms/gallery-308-building-a.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Jelena Laketić](../../people/jelena-laketic.md)

Join Jelena Laketić, a distinguished engineer, as she shares the journey of adopting GitHub Copilot at Thomson Reuters. Learn how the initiative moved from initial assessment to adoption, rolling out Copilot to more than 2,000 software developers across the enterprise. Jelena will share the challenges, successes, and lessons learned along the way, including how to evaluate Copilot's capabilities and measure the impact on engineering excellence and the developer experience. You'll walk away with insights, tips, and best practices to inform and inspire your own journey with Copilot.
