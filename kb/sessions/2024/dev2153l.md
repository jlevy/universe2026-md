# Taking your developer experience from 0 to 1 with Atlassian engineering leaders

**Tue Oct 29 2024, 1:30 PM–2:10 PM PDT** · [Discussions Lounge](../../rooms/discussions-lounge-firehouse.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Preeti Kota](../../people/preeti-kota.md) (facilitator), [Andrew Boyagi](../../people/andrew-boyagi.md) (facilitator)

**Hosted by:** [Atlassian](../../organizations/atlassian.md)

The key to delivering high quality software at speed is to provide a positive developer experience. Did you know that 69% of developers lose over eight hours a week due to inefficiencies, and most believe their leaders are clueless about the core issues? Join Atlassian's Andrew Boyagi, head of DevOps Evangelism, and Preeti Kota, head of Engineering for Compass, for a discussion on how to up-level your developer experience to accelerate velocity and improve compliance. Andrew and Preeti will also share best practices learned at Atlassian.

- **Type:** Discussion Lounge
- **Track:** Developer Experience
- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
- **Topics:** Agile practices, AI, Cloud, DevOps, DevSecOps, Software Engineering, Collaboration, Productivity
- **GitHub products:** Copilot, Extensibility/Ecosystem, Sponsors
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developers
