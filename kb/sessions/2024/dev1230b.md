# How the New York Times engineered a user-friendly developer platform

**Wed Oct 30 2024, 10:30 AM–11:10 AM PDT** · [Cowell Theater](../../rooms/cowell-theater-gateway-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [David Grizzanti](../../people/david-grizzanti.md)

Accelerating time to market is paramount and engineers play a pivotal role. The key to staying ahead is to build an Internal Developer Platform (IDP) centered around self-service tooling, which leads to better developer and end-user experiences.

In this session, David Grizzanti, principal engineer at The New York Times, will explore the nuances of platform engineering and offer insights for using GitHub as a foundation. He'll also share some specific challenges faced when designing the New York Times Developer Platform and those challenges that were addressed, ultimately enabling developers to deliver everything from breaking news to the next viral game.

- **Type:** Breakout Session
- **Track:** Developer Experience
- **Level:** Level 200: Intermediate
- **Topics:** Automated Infrastructure Deployment, Cloud, Continuous Integration + Continuous Deployment (CI/CD), Containerized Applications, DevOps, Kubernetes, Infrastructure as Code, Monitoring and Observability, Collaboration, Productivity
- **GitHub products:** Advanced Security, Copilot, Discussions, Enterprise/Admin, Extensibility/Ecosystem
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developers, Open Source Developers or Maintainers
- **Mentions:** [The New York Times](../../organizations/the-new-york-times.md)
