# Dungeons and deployments: The clusters of chaos

**Wed Oct 30 2024, 2:45 PM–3:25 PM PDT** · [Main Stage](../../rooms/main-stage-festival-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Natali Vlatko](../../people/natali-vlatko.md) (panelist), [Seth McCombs](../../people/seth-mccombs.md) (panelist), [Noah Abrahams](../../people/noah-abrahams.md) (moderator), [Ian Coldwater](../../people/ian-coldwater.md) (panelist), [Kat Cosgrove](../../people/kat-cosgrove.md) (panelist)

You haven't mastered the basics of Kubernetes until you have a grasp on its security concerns. Join our adventurers as we journey to the “Duchy d'Voups” in search of knowledge, glory, and the “Weregoose” who stole the “Holy Certificate.”  We'll need to find ways to buff our defenses, guard our camp, and watch each other's backs, all while making sure the “Weregoose” doesn't learn our secrets. Whether you're a “paladin,” a “rogue,” or a “sorcerer,” there's a way you can help keep your cluster secure. Roll for initiative: your cluster is under attack.

This session with Noah Abrahams, consultant; Ian Coldwater, senior principal security architect at Docker; Kat Cosgrove, a lead open source advocate; Natali Vlatko, open source architect at Cisco; and Seth McCombs, senior infrastructure engineer at AcuityMD promises to be fun.

- **Type:** Main Stage
- **Track:** Developer Experience, Security
- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
- **Topics:** Cloud, Containerized Applications, DevOps, DevSecOps, Kubernetes, Security, Supply Chain Security, Threat Modeling
- **GitHub products:** Supply Chain Security, Education, Extensibility/Ecosystem, Projects and Issues
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Developers, Open Source Developers or Maintainers, Security Professionals, Security Leadership
- **Mentions:** [Kubernetes](../../software/kubernetes.yaml), [Docker](../../software/docker.yaml), [Cisco](../../organizations/cisco.md), [AcuityMD](../../organizations/acuitymd.md)
