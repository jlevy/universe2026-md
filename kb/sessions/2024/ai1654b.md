# The real deal: EY professionals share how to prove the value of GitHub Copilot

**Tue Oct 29 2024, 12:30 PM–1:10 PM PDT** · [Cowell Theater](../../rooms/cowell-theater-gateway-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Zeba Khan](../../people/zeba-khan.md), [Debolin Dhar](../../people/debolin-dhar.md)

GitHub Copilot is revolutionizing software development and transforming the SDLC around the developer experience. Yet it's challenging for CIOs to measure the return on investment (ROI). In this session, Debolin Dhar, technology architect manager, and Zeba Khan, technology strategy and transformation senior manager, will share how Ernst & Young (EY) built a framework to understand the metrics that most effectively measure Copilot benefits, enabling organizations to accurately calculate and communicate the ROI of Copilot. 

Debolin and Zeba will also share how EY supported one of America’s biggest banks to measure Copilot ROI with metrics and dashboards, highlighting the developer experience as a key enabler for overall success.
