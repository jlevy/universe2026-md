# A little time saved goes a long way: AMD’s AI story

**Tue Oct 29 2024, 11:30 AM–12:10 PM PDT** · [Cowell Theater](../../rooms/cowell-theater-gateway-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Alexander Androncik](../../people/alexander-androncik.md)

The best companies and engineering teams focus on improving engineering efficiency to effectively defy time. Working a few hours faster each day gives you back a few extra days each month or months each year. When this valuable time is applied to innovation, it leads to outstanding results. Join Alexander Androncik, senior director of software development at AMD, to hear more about AMD's journey with AI and how it improves the developer experience and helps find more time for developers to create tangible business value.
