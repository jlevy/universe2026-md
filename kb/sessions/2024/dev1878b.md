# An engineer’s guide to starting your company

**Tue Oct 29 2024, 1:30 PM–2:10 PM PDT** · [Gallery 308](../../rooms/gallery-308-building-a.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Dani Grant](../../people/dani-grant.md)

This session aims to provide a step-by-step guide for engineers who want to start a company, raise your first million, and gain your first 1,000 customers. 

Hear from Dani Grant, Jam CEO, about everything Jam has learned—from day zero to raising over $10 million and earning 125,000+ users. She'll break down each step along the path to growth and share actual examples of Jam's email outreach to venture capitalists (VCs), fundraising pitch decks, and outbound messages aimed at early users.

In this session, you'll see how engineers have the power to change the world through software, and Dani will make sure you’re armed with information you need to raise capital, attract users, and go forth to build your startup.

- **Type:** Breakout Session
- **Track:** Developer Experience
- **Level:** Level 200: Intermediate
- **Topics:** Human Factors, Software Engineering, Collaboration, Community
- **GitHub products:** Copilot, Pages, Projects and Issues
- **Audience:** Startups
- **Mentions:** [Jam](../../organizations/jam.md)
