# How to measure the effectiveness of GitHub Copilot

**Wed Oct 30 2024, 2:30 PM–2:55 PM PDT** · [Demo Studio](../../rooms/demo-studio-gateway-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Mickey Gousset](../../people/mickey-gousset.md), [Kitty Chiu](../../people/kitty-chiu.md)

Developers using GitHub Copilot know its value, but how do you show that value to get continued buy-in from the business? How do you know if you're measuring what matters most, and how do you use those metrics to craft a story for executives? If these questions keep you up at night, this session is for you. 

Join GitHub's Senior DevOps Architect, Kitty Chiu, and Staff DevOps Architect, Mickey Gousset, for a walk through the Copilot Metrics API. You'll learn how to tell your Copilot-powered productivity story and get additional support and investment from the business.

- **Level:** Level 200: Intermediate
