# Complex migrations: Why failing forward helps push past resistance to change

**Tue Oct 29 2024, 10:30 AM–11:10 AM PDT** · [Cowell Theater](../../rooms/cowell-theater-gateway-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Richard Bullington-McGuire](../../people/richard-bullington-mcguire.md), [Mark Quigley](../../people/mark-quigley.md), [Sam Gaudet](../../people/sam-gaudet.md)

Change is hard, even when you know the change is worth it. When Wayfair moved to GitHub Enterprise Cloud in 2023, the task of moving 15,000+ repositories and 3,000 active users was daunting. There were numerous security, network, and operational challenges to surmount, but with careful preparation our migration ran smoothly. 

We're releasing the migration toolkit Wayfair developed as open source to make it easier for others to do the same. Join us to learn how we pushed our teams to embrace discomfort and adopt a fail-forward approach, even on our most sensitive code base.

- **GitHub products:** Enterprise/Admin, Extensibility/Ecosystem
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developers, Security Leadership
