# Putting product requirements within easy reach using AI

**Tue Oct 29 2024, 4:30 PM–4:55 PM PDT** · [Demo Stage](../../rooms/demo-stage-gateway-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Melissa Miller](../../people/melissa-miller.md)

Developers deliver what customers need according to product requirements. Yet they often lose time during development by switching between Jira and VSCode to ensure that the code delivers the necessary requirements.

This is where AI can help—it can return hours of productivity to teams by bringing in the business context from Jira and Confluence during code development and when chatting with GitHub Copilot. In this session, Melissa Miller, head of Product Marketing, AI at Atlassian, will share how you can integrate Atlassian and Copilot to streamline the development flow and leverage Rovo (Atlassian’s AI agents) to better manage requirements and knowledge. This makes it easier and faster for developers to get the information they need to build features within their IDE.
