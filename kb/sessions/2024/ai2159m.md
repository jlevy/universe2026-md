# AI-native product development lifecycle: Disrupting how software is developed today

**Wed Oct 30 2024, 11:15 AM–11:45 AM PDT** · [Main Stage](../../rooms/main-stage-festival-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Chandrasekar Gnanasambandam](../../people/chandrasekar-gnanasambandam.md), [Rikki Singh](../../people/rikki-singh.md), [Martin Harrysson](../../people/martin-harrysson.md)

There is currently an increased focus on developer code companion tools. But what happens when all roles across the product development lifecycle adopt GenAI tools tailored to their needs? Does it change the way we build software? Does it change the talent profiles software companies hire? Can it help build better products for customers? During this session, leaders from McKinsey & Company will share insights from their research across leading technology companies on what the perceived impact and implications are.

- **Track:** AI
- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
- **Topics:** Agile practices, AI, Security, Software Engineering, Testing and related practices, Productivity
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developers, Startups, Security Leadership, Educators
- **Mentions:** [McKinsey & Company](../../organizations/mckinsey-company.md)
