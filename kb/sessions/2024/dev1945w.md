# Promotion unlocked: Level up your career and expand your influence

**Tue Oct 29 2024, 12:30 PM–2:00 PM PDT** · [Workshops](../../rooms/workshops-general-s-residence.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Taylor Blau](../../people/taylor-blau.md) (facilitator), [Marion Lang](../../people/marion-lang.md) (facilitator)

If you've been thinking about transitioning into a principal/staff engineering role, or wonder how to even make this shift, this workshop is for you. Join GitHub's Marion Lang, principal software engineer, and Taylor Blau, staff software engineer, for this GitHub's Experts workshop designed to help you grow beyond an individual contributor and into a position of technical leadership. Marion and Taylor will explore the must-have skills for any successful principal/staff engineer and promise that if you attend with a growth mindset, you'll leave with a tangible plan for next leveling your career. As a bonus, you'll also meet a lot of other growth-oriented engineers.

- **Type:** Workshop
- **Track:** Developer Experience
- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
- **Topics:** Human Factors, Collaboration, Community
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Developers, Open Source Developers or Maintainers, Security Professionals
