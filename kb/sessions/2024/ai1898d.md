# In case you missed it: Powerful AI agents and AI-native workflows across GitHub

**Wed Oct 30 2024, 3:30 PM–3:55 PM PDT** · [Demo Studio](../../rooms/demo-studio-gateway-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Tim Rogers](../../people/tim-rogers.md)

GitHub Copilot has a simple goal: to increase developer productivity and accelerate the pace of software development. 

Copilot started its journey in the IDE, but GitHub's research reveals that the average developer only spends about two hours a day coding. That's why GitHub is evolving to bring more Copilot experiences front and center, with powerful AI agents and customizations for your organizational knowledge. 

In this fast-paced demo, Tim Rogers, senior product manager at GitHub, will show how GitHub is infusing AI across the platform in new and magical ways to boost developer productivity and bring small moments of delight to their day.
