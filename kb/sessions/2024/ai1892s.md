# Take your AI extension to new heights with JetBrains IDE capabilities

**Wed Oct 30 2024, 1:30 PM–2:10 PM PDT** · [Sandbox Stage](../../rooms/sandbox-stage-general-s-residence.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Jen Agarwal](../../people/jen-agarwal.md) (facilitator), [Thomas Sickert](../../people/thomas-sickert.md) (facilitator)

Join GitHub's Jen Agarwal, staff product manager, and Thomas Sickert, software engineer, as they explore how to unlock the full potential of your AI extension by integrating with JetBrains' IDEs. By leveraging rich code context, project structure, and the UI capabilities ingrained in the editor, you can enhance your extension capabilities, improve code suggestion accuracy, and elevate the developer experience. 

Note: This stage has limited capacity and will seat attendees at a first come first serve basis.  Adding this session to your schedule does not reserve a seat.  Please attend early.
