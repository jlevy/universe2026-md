# Merging made fast and easy: Demystifying GitHub's merge queue

**Tue Oct 29 2024, 2:00 PM–2:25 PM PDT** · [Demo Stage](../../rooms/demo-stage-gateway-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Josh Cannon](../../people/josh-cannon.md)

There's no need to choose between evergreen mainline, efficient merging, or a handful of CI builds. Now, you can tackle all three. In this session, Josh Cannon, member of technical staff at Anthropic, will dive deep into GitHub's merge queue and how to crank it to 11 to streamline your team's processes. Josh will walk through what the merge queue is, the problems it solves, how it works, and call out some potential "gotcha's" with solutions for mitigating them. You can expect to see some demos, have some fun, and walk away with the power to become a merge queue maestro.

- **Type:** Product Demo
- **Track:** Developer Experience
- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
- **Topics:** Continuous Integration + Continuous Deployment (CI/CD), Git and related code management techniques, Software Engineering, Testing and related practices
- **GitHub products:** Actions
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Developers, Open Source Developers or Maintainers
- **Mentions:** [Anthropic](../../organizations/anthropic.md)
