# GitHub’s Communities of Belonging Mixer

**Tue Oct 29 2024, 5:30 PM–6:30 PM PDT** · [Coastal Club](../../rooms/coastal-club-greens.yaml)

We're bringing Universe attendees and GitHub's Communities of Belonging members together for an hour of networking and celebration. The Communities of Belonging are employee-led, globally distributed groups, where members with shared identities, and allies, work together to champion efforts around diversity, inclusion, and belonging. These efforts support GitHub employees, the community, and drive GitHub's mission to build a more inclusive developer ecosystem.

Whether you identify as part of one or many Communities, or as an ally, come enjoy some good bites, beverages, and conversation at the Coastal Club at Greens. You'll also have the option to make a donation to any of the Community of Belonging Benevity funds. Limited space is available, please add this event to your agenda to reserve your spot.

- **Type:** Special Event
- **Track:** AI, Developer Experience, Security
