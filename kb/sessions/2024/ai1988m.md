# Unlocking AI at scale: Crafting a compliant and high-impact AI strategy

**Wed Oct 30 2024, 10:15 AM–10:55 AM PDT** · [Main Stage](../../rooms/main-stage-festival-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Shelley McKinley](../../people/shelley-mckinley.md), [Elizabeth Pemmerl](../../people/elizabeth-pemmerl.md), [Sandhya Sridharan](../../people/sandhya-sridharan.md)

AI is quickly becoming essential for enterprises to drive growth and achieve business goals. However, companies in regulated industries face additional complexities when scaling AI. In this session, gain insights from a chief information officer, chief legal officer, and chief revenue officer on designing and executing a clear AI roadmap that drives adoption, supports compliance, and delivers strong ROI. Learn how JPMorgan Chase built an AI strategy rooted in delivering a world-class developer experience with GitHub Copilot to accelerate developer velocity and create value.
