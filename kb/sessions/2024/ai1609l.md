# Developer’s Cafe: Let's chat and co-create the future of development with AI

**Tue Oct 29 2024, 4:30 PM–5:10 PM PDT** · [Discussions Lounge](../../rooms/discussions-lounge-firehouse.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Jenny Hwang](../../people/jenny-hwang.md) (facilitator), [Julie Stuart](../../people/julie-stuart.md) (facilitator), [Ya Gao](../../people/ya-gao.md) (facilitator)

Some of the best conversations happen over coffee. That's why we're inviting you to the "Developer's Cafe," facilitated by Jenny Hwang, Julie Stuart, and Ya Gao, all from the user research team at GitHub. We all have a hand in co-creating the future of development with AI, and this space is designed for intimate conversations with fellow attendees to discuss how we can influence our best-case scenarios for using AI in software development.

- **Type:** Discussion Lounge
- **Track:** AI, Developer Experience
- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
- **Topics:** AI, Human Factors, Software Engineering, Collaboration, Community
- **GitHub products:** Actions, Advanced Security, Codespaces, Copilot, Extensibility/Ecosystem
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developers, Startups
