# Scaling CD with Octopus Deploy and GitHub Copilot

**Tue Oct 29 2024, 2:30 PM–3:10 PM PDT** · [Sandbox Stage](../../rooms/sandbox-stage-general-s-residence.yaml)

**Speakers:** [James Chatmas](../../people/james-chatmas.md) (facilitator)

**Hosted by:** [Octopus Deploy](../../organizations/octopus-deploy.md)

How do you make scaling CD fast but flexible for your enterprise? It requires disciplined platform engineering practices. In this interactive sandbox session, James Chatmas, principal solutions architect at Octopus Deploy, will demonstrate how platform teams can leverage Octopus Deploy and GitHub Copilot to automate the creation and management of deployment projects, reduce duplication of effort, and ensure consistency across environments. Join James for a live demo, followed by practical exercises that will solidify your understanding of how to implement these practices in your own organization.
