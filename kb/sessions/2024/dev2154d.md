# Improve your developer experience with Compass, Atlassian's internal developer platform

**Wed Oct 30 2024, 11:30 AM–11:55 AM PDT** · [Demo Studio](../../rooms/demo-studio-gateway-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Raghu Venkatesh](../../people/raghu-venkatesh.md)

**Hosted by:** [Atlassian](../../organizations/atlassian.md)

As software engineering grows more complex and developers shoulder increasing amounts of responsibility, organizations are investing in platform engineering initiatives to enable their teams to run at full speed. Join Atlassian’s Raghu Venkatesh, senior engineer manager, to learn more about Atlassian’s internal developer platform, Compass, built to serve the needs of platform engineering teams. See how Compass empowers platform teams to enhance the developer experience, improve compliance, and accelerate engineering velocity with capabilities like a software component catalog, templates, scorecards, and more.

- **Type:** Product Demo
- **Track:** Developer Experience
- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
- **Topics:** Continuous Integration + Continuous Deployment (CI/CD), Data Analytics, DevOps, DevSecOps, Software Engineering, Collaboration, Tech Debt
- **GitHub products:** Actions, Enterprise/Admin, Extensibility/Ecosystem, Sponsors
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developers
