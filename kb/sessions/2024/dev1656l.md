# Bridging the gap: Building a strong connection between business and product development

**Tue Oct 29 2024, 12:30 PM–1:10 PM PDT** · [Discussions Lounge](../../rooms/discussions-lounge-firehouse.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Chris Alex Bauer](../../people/chris-alex-bauer.md) (facilitator)

Step into an immersive group collaboration experience at the Discussions Lounge, where we'll divide into two teams to determine whether business or product development should take the initiative, tackling the age-old debate: what comes first, the chicken or the egg? We'll role-play as business and product teams and work to find common ground. We'll also learn to identify when developers should take the first step to bridge the gap between business development and product development. Through dynamic discussions and interactive problem solving, we'll explore effective strategies for fostering cross-team collaboration, aligning priorities, maintaining clear communication, and driving successful outcomes.

- **Type:** Discussion Lounge
- **Track:** Developer Experience
- **Level:** Level 200: Intermediate
- **Topics:** Agile practices, Human Factors, Social Impact, Collaboration, Community, Productivity
- **GitHub products:** Discussions, Pages, Projects and Issues
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developers, Security Professionals, Startups, Security Leadership, Educators
