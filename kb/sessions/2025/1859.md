**Wed Oct 29 2025, 3:10 PM–3:35 PM PDT** · [GitHub Central](../../rooms/github-central-festival-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [James M Beyers](../../people/james-m-beyers.md), [Irene Modeste](../../people/irene-modeste.md), [Mitch Enderle](../../people/mitch-enderle.md)

Whether you're curious about stakeholder buy-in, technical hurdles, change management at scale, or measuring success across thousands of developers—bring your questions and get real answers from the people who lived through it.
