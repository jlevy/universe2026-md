# New Relic Happy Hour at GitHub Universe 2025

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 5:30 PM–8:30 PM PDT** · [Cartoon Art Museum](../../rooms/cartoon-art-museum-781-beach-street.yaml)

Coming to GitHub Universe? Join us for happy hour on Tuesday, Oct 28 from 5:30-8:30pm at the Cartoon Art Museum where you’ll be able to meet the engineers behind the latest New Relic + GitHub Copilot integration.

You’ll also be able to get exclusive access to the Batman Armory at the museum. Take a selfie with the batsuit worn by Michael Keaton in 1989’s Batman; the Val Kilmer Panther Batman and Sonar Batman suits; the George Clooney Batman; Christian Bale's Batman from The Dark Knight; and the Ben Affleck Bat Armor from Batman vs. Superman.

The museum is a 20 min walk or a 5 min Uber ride from where the event is being held in Fort Mason. We’ll provide a $10 Uber gift card to everyone who attends.

See you there for a live demo, Q&A, food, drinks, swag, and some pretty cool cartoon art.
