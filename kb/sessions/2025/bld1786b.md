# When AI meets millions of developers: Evaluating LLMs for Copilot

**Wed Oct 29 2025, 11:15 AM–11:55 AM PDT** · [BATS Theatre](../../rooms/bats-theatre-fort-mason-building-b.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Judge Hiciano](../../people/judge-hiciano.md)

What happens when large language models go from the lab to IDEs and products serving millions of developers? In this talk, you'll go behind the scenes of GitHub Copilot’s evaluation journey, from early prototypes to full-fledged product releases across GitHub. You’ll see how our team built a rigorous evaluation stack that blends offline benchmarks, online experiments, and real-world telemetry to answer one of the toughest questions in our space: Is this actually helping developers?

You'll get to explore how we balanced speed with safety, innovation with reliability, and intuition with data, all while shaping the next generation of intelligent developer tools. Expect candid lessons, insights, and maybe even a few "don’t try this at home" stories. If you’ve ever wondered how we evaluate LLMs at GitHub, or just want to see what it takes to make AI useful at planetary scale, this session is for you.

- **GitHub products:** GitHub Copilot, VS Code
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Developer, Open Source Developer or Maintainer
