# Ship & Tell: General Motors

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 3:35 PM–4:00 PM PDT** · [GitHub Central](../../rooms/github-central-festival-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Mario Parisi](../../people/mario-parisi.md), [Kal Willard](../../people/kal-willard.md)

Curious how you transform software development at a 100+ year-old engineering powerhouse? General Motors [just shared their journey from fragmented tools to a unified GitHub platform](https://reg.githubuniverse.com/flow/github/universe25/attendee-portal/page/sessioncatalog/session/1752876361950001R1DX). Now it's your turn to ask how they pulled it off. Join the team from GM for an open Q&A where you can dive into the details of their modernization roadmap, from navigating cultural shifts and winning over skeptics to implementing AI-assisted workflows and securing code at scale. Whether you're wrestling with legacy systems, building the business case for platform consolidation, or figuring out how to measure developer productivity gains—bring your toughest questions about large-scale transformation.

- **Type:** Ship & Tell
- **Topics:** Migration strategies
- **GitHub products:** GitHub Advanced Security, GitHub Copilot
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developer, Security Professional, Security Leadership
- **Mentions:** [General Motors](../../organizations/general-motors.md)
