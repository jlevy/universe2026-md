# Ship & Tell: Duke Energy

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 11:30 AM–11:55 AM PDT** · [GitHub Central](../../rooms/github-central-festival-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Kal Schumacher](../../people/kal-schumacher.md)

Curious how Duke Energy transformed siloed teams into a unified developer community that powered their platform migration and AI adoption? Kal Schumacher [just walked through their journey](https://reg.githubuniverse.com/flow/github/universe25/attendee-portal/page/sessioncatalog/session/1746130182247001EN9p)—now he's here to answer your questions. Join him for an open Q&A where you can dig into the practical side of community building at scale. Whether you're trying to break down team silos, figure out how to amplify developer voices in CI/CD decisions, navigate a multi-tool migration without disrupting productivity, scale innersource practices across the enterprise, or get teams actually excited about AI-native development—bring your questions about turning organizational transformation from a mandate into a movement.

- **Type:** Ship & Tell
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership
- **Mentions:** [Duke Energy](../../organizations/duke-energy.md)
