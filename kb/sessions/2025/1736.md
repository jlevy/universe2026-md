# Give Back at Universe: Build Care Kits with GitHub Gives & Xebia Cares

**Wed Oct 29 2025, 12:35 PM–1:35 PM PDT** · [Discussions Lounge](../../rooms/discussions-lounge-fort-mason-building-d.yaml)

Join GitHub’s Social Impact team in partnership with Xebia to assemble 400 care kits for individuals experiencing homelessness in San Francisco. These kits will be donated to local nonprofit GLIDE and distributed during their community outreach walks. With tables for packing essentials and writing heartfelt cards, this volunteer activity invites all conference attendees to pause, connect, and make a difference together. Your participation helps support the local community while fostering connection and shared purpose at Universe.

- **Type:** Social
