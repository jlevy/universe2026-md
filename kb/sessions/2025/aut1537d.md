# AI-driven testing: Scaling automation with KaneAI and HyperExecute

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 11:10 AM–11:25 AM PDT** · [Demos & Donuts](../../rooms/demos-donuts-gateway-pavilion-level-2.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Mayank Bhola](../../people/mayank-bhola.md)

**Hosted by:** [TestMu AI](../../organizations/testmu-ai.md)

Get ready to take your test automation to the next level with KaneAI and HyperExecute from LambdaTest. In this session, you'll discover how the LambdaTest team integrated their AI-powered workflows with GitHub Actions to speed up releases by 64%, boost test reliability above 90%, and cut audit prep time by 70% — all while delivering resilient automation and increased visibility at scale.

You’ll learn how to plan and write tests in natural language, then run them up to 70% faster across thousands of environments, without changing your current CI/CD pipelines.

- **Track:** Build, Automate
- **Level:** Level 200: Intermediate
- **Topics:** AI (Agentic AI, Generative AI, Fine-tuned modeling, Models), DevOps and DevSecOps, Testing and related practices
- **GitHub products:** Actions, GitHub Copilot, VS Code
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developer, Open Source Developer or Maintainer, Startups
