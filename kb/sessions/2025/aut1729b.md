# Brick by brick: Building developer experience at the LEGO Group

**Wed Oct 29 2025, 11:15 AM–11:55 AM PDT** · [Cowell Theater](../../rooms/cowell-theater-gateway-pavilion-level-1.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Rasmus Hald](../../people/rasmus-hald.md)

How do you grow a high‑trust developer culture at scale, especially in a company where creativity and play are part of the DNA? At The LEGO Group, developer experience isn’t just a platform or a toolkit; it’s a community practice. Rasmus shares how intentional rituals, innersource patterns, and a focus on well‑being turned fragmented efforts into a cohesive, empowered engineering ecosystem. You’ll see how playful practices can coexist with disciplined engineering standards, and how shared ownership increases reuse and lowers onboarding friction. Walk away with adaptable ideas to strengthen belonging, accelerate knowledge flow, and help your own engineers feel connected, motivated, and proud of what they build.

- **Type:** Breakout Session
- **Track:** Build, Automate
- **Level:** Level 200: Intermediate
- **Topics:** Agile practices, AI (Agentic AI, Generative AI, Fine-tuned modeling, Models), DevOps and DevSecOps, Software Engineering, Collaboration
- **GitHub products:** GitHub Copilot, Enterprise/Admin, Issues
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developer
- **Mentions:** [the LEGO Group](../../organizations/the-lego-group.md)
