# Your codebase, your rules: Customizing Copilot with context engineering

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 2:30 PM–2:45 PM PDT** · [Silent Disco Demos](../../rooms/silent-disco-demos-festival-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Harald Kirschner](../../people/harald-kirschner.md)

Join us to unlock the full potential of GitHub Copilot in VS Code into a deeply context-aware pair programmer that understands not just your specific codebase, but also your team's workflows. Learn how to combine custom instructions, prompts, MCPs, and context engineering principles to give Copilot insight into your project structure, architectural decisions, and coding patterns. Walk away with a practical framework for scaling context-aware workflows across enterprise codebases, along with ready-to-use templates for project-wide context and plan-specific instructions.

- **Track:** Build
- **Level:** Level 300: Advanced
- **Topics:** AI (Agentic AI, Generative AI, Fine-tuned modeling, Models)
- **GitHub products:** GitHub Copilot, VS Code
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Developer, Open Source Developer or Maintainer
