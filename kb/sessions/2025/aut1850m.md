# Empowering business leaders to scale innovation in the age of AI

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 11:15 AM–11:45 AM PDT** · [Main Stage](../../rooms/main-stage-festival-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Mario Rodriguez](../../people/mario-rodriguez.md), [Todd Manion](../../people/todd-manion.md), [Kate Catlin](../../people/kate-catlin.md)

Agentic AI isn’t a trend - it’s a shift in how software is created. The next wave of innovation won’t come just from developers, but from the collaboration between humans and agents. In this session, GitHub’s Chief Product Officer Mario Rodriguez shares how we’re expanding GitHub’s AI-powered Developer Platform for this new, AI-native SDLC. Through live demos, you’ll see how teams can move at AI speed while staying secure, visible, and in control.

- **Type:** Main Stage
- **Track:** Automate
- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership
