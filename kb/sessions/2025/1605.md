# Exclusive Executive Networking Dinner with LambdaTest

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 6:00 PM–9:00 PM PDT** · [Roma Antica](../../rooms/roma-antica-3242-scott-st.yaml)

This gathering is designed to bring together senior leaders in technology and quality engineering for insightful discussions and networking.
