# Building accessible software with continuous AI

**Wed Oct 29 2025, 12:55 PM–1:10 PM PDT** · [Demos & Donuts](../../rooms/demos-donuts-gateway-pavilion-level-2.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Ed Summers](../../people/ed-summers.md)

One in seven people worldwide lives with a disability. If your software isn’t accessible, you’re leaving users behind. In this session, we’ll show you how continuous AI for accessibility integrates inclusion into your development process from day one. You’ll learn how GitHub’s open source accessibility scanner can help identify and address issues early — enabling improved experiences for a broader range of users, while minimizing disruption to your workflow.

- **Type:** Product Demo
- **Track:** Build
- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
- **Topics:** Accessibility, Open source, Social impact, Testing and related practices
- **GitHub products:** Actions, GitHub Copilot
- **Audience:** Open Source Developer or Maintainer
