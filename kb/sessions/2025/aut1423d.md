# Taking control of enterprise costs with GitHub’s new billing platform

**Wed Oct 29 2025, 3:10 PM–3:25 PM PDT** · [Demos & Donuts](../../rooms/demos-donuts-gateway-pavilion-level-2.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Sowmya Vivek](../../people/sowmya-vivek.md), [Greg Mondello](../../people/greg-mondello.md)

Managing billing and licensing across an enterprise can be complex — but it doesn’t have to be. In this session, you’ll get an inside look at GitHub’s new enterprise billing and licensing platform and how it gives you greater control and visibility over your organization’s expenses.

We’ll show you how to use features like cost centers, usage reports, and budget controls to better manage spending, track trends, and make smarter, data-driven decisions. You’ll walk away with practical tips to streamline billing, improve efficiency, and keep your costs in check — without slowing your team down.

- **Type:** Product Demo
- **Track:** Automate
- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
- **Topics:** Data (analytics, management, privacy), Custom integrations and APIs
- **GitHub products:** GitHub Advanced Security, GitHub Copilot, GitHub Enterprise Cloud
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership
