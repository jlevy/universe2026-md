# Agents assemble! The power of AI tools that work together

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 12:50 PM–1:05 PM PDT** · [Silent Disco Demos](../../rooms/silent-disco-demos-festival-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Jemiah Sius](../../people/jemiah-sius.md)

**Hosted by:** [New Relic](../../organizations/new-relic.md)

Most of us are familiar with generative AI (think: ChatGPT), but we’re still constantly switching between multiple tools to finish a project. Now, imagine AI assistants that are able to not just execute tasks, but also collaborate with each other. 

In this session, you'll get an inside look on this development in AI, called agentic AI — systems that can autonomously plan, reason, and act to achieve specific goals. You'll explore how AI agents like New Relic and GitHub Copilot communicate, make decisions, and share data that would otherwise remain locked in separate systems. You’ll see a high-level overview of the architecture behind these agent-to-agent interactions and the practical protocols that enable them to coordinate effectively. 

You’ll also learn how agentic systems solve real problems, from debugging complex applications to automating entire development workflows. Whether you’re new to coding or a seasoned developer, join our session to discover how this technology can dramatically reduce context-switching and cognitive load.
