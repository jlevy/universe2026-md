# How to give GitHub the right context with Copilot Spaces

**Wed Oct 29 2025, 4:20 PM–4:35 PM PDT** · [Silent Disco Demos](../../rooms/silent-disco-demos-festival-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Vanessa Kalu](../../people/vanessa-kalu.md)

Ever wish GitHub Copilot just understood your project the first time, without needing to re-explain context or dig through scattered docs? Copilot Spaces is your shortcut. In this fast-paced demo, Product Manager Vanessa Kalu will show you how Spaces puts the right context—files, pull requests, issues, and repos—at Copilot’s fingertips. See how Spaces helps you onboard faster, solve problems without context gaps, and finally stop chasing info across tools. We’ll create a Space, add persistent and live-updating context, and share it securely with teammates, all in a few clicks. You’ll leave ready to try Spaces yourself and see how much better Copilot can be when it’s grounded in the right context.
