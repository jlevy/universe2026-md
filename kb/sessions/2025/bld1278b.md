# From MVP to GitHub.com: An AI story of engineering and product collaboration

**Wed Oct 29 2025, 3:15 PM–3:55 PM PDT** · [Cowell Theater](../../rooms/cowell-theater-gateway-pavilion-level-1.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Anjuan Simmons](../../people/anjuan-simmons.md)

Journey through the evolution of GitHub’s AI‑powered productivity dashboard from proof of concept to a core element of the GitHub homepage. This session shows how cross-functional collaboration among engineering, product, and design transformed an MVP—leveraging Copilot‑generated insights—into a robust productivity tool. Learn how the team tackled technical constraints, scaled the solution, and navigated the interplay of engineering and product management. Discover practical strategies for implementing AI features that enhance developer productivity while building user trust.

- **Type:** Breakout Session
- **Track:** Build, Automate
- **Level:** Level 200: Intermediate
- **Topics:** Agile practices, AI (Agentic AI, Generative AI, Fine-tuned modeling, Models), Collaboration, Productivity
- **GitHub products:** GitHub Copilot, Projects and Issues
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developer
- **Mentions:** [GitHub Copilot](../../software/github-copilot.yaml)
