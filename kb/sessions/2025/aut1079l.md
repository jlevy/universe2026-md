# Leading with GitHub Copilot: Rethinking experience, strategy, and collaboration

**Wed Oct 29 2025, 2:00 PM–3:00 PM PDT** · [Discussions Lounge](../../rooms/discussions-lounge-fort-mason-building-d.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Vicky Israni](../../people/vicky-israni.md) (facilitator), [Kartik Gupta](../../people/kartik-gupta.md) (facilitator)

It's time to unlock the power of GitHub Copilot beyond the IDE. Join fellow product owners, managers, designers, and technical leaders in an interactive discussion about how AI is transforming non-development workflows.

Come ready to share your experiences with AI tools in planning, documentation, and technical decision-making. Together, we'll explore how teams are using GitHub Copilot for streamlining user stories, requirements gathering, and strategic planning tasks. Participants will collaborate on practical approaches, share implementation challenges, and discuss organizational change strategies around AI adoption in leadership roles.

Bring your own use cases and pain points — you'll learn from diverse team experiences and leave with actionable strategies informed by peer insights.

- **Type:** Discussions Lounge
- **Track:** Automate
- **Level:** Level 200: Intermediate
- **Topics:** Agile practices, AI (Agentic AI, Generative AI, Fine-tuned modeling, Models), Collaboration
- **GitHub products:** GitHub Copilot
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership
- **Mentions:** [GitHub Copilot](../../software/github-copilot.yaml)
