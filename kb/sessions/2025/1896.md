# Ship & Tell: Commonwealth Bank of Australia

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 12:50 PM–1:15 PM PDT** · [GitHub Central](../../rooms/github-central-festival-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Helen Lau](../../people/helen-lau.md), [Nathan Chandler](../../people/nathan-chandler.md)

Want to know what it really takes to roll out secret protection across thousands of repos without grinding development to a halt? Commonwealth Bank of Australia [just walked through their partnership with GitHub to tackle one of security's toughest challenges](https://reg.githubuniverse.com/flow/github/universe25/attendee-portal/page/sessioncatalog/session/1759150344212001smkx)—now they're here to answer your questions. Join the CBA team for an open Q&A where you can dig into the practical details of their "get clean, stay clean" approach. Whether you're struggling with developer adoption, figuring out how to prioritize remediation at scale, balancing security guardrails with velocity, or building the automation that makes it all sustainable—bring your questions about turning enterprise secret management from a nightmare into a working system.

- **Mentions:** [Commonwealth Bank](../../organizations/commonwealth-bank.md)
