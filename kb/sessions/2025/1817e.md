# GitHub HQ Day of Learning Experience

**Thu Oct 30 2025, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM PDT** · [Level 1](../../rooms/level-1-github-hq.yaml)

Step inside GitHub headquarters for an exclusive, one-day experience designed to inspire, challenge, and celebrate the developer community. On October 30, 2025, Universe attendees will have the opportunity to earn certifications, sharpen their expertise through Applied Skills labs, and take part in hands-on competitions powered by GitHub Spark.

Throughout the day, enjoy engaging product demos, interactive sessions like the GitHub Well-Architected Framework, and friendly competitions judged by GitHub employees. Tours of HQ, raffles, delicious food, and plenty of chances to connect with peers and leaders make this a truly one-of-a-kind event.

Whether you’re here to validate your skills, explore new tools, or experience the culture of GitHub up close, this is your chance to immerse yourself in learning and community at the heart of GitHub Universe. Space is limited, so secure your spot early!
