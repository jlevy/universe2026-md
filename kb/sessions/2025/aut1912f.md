# Copilot rollout strategy: A developer experience story

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 4:30 PM–5:00 PM PDT** · [Cowell Theater](../../rooms/cowell-theater-gateway-pavilion-level-1.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Gully Patel](../../people/gully-patel.md) (panelist), [Daniel Connolly](../../people/daniel-connolly.md) (moderator)

This fireside chat explores the strategic rollout of GitHub Copilot across a global developer ecosystem. Framed through five key themes—Meticulousness, Responsibility, Customer Focus, Scale, and Metrics—the conversation highlights how technical precision, governance, and empathy shaped a transformative experience. Learn how the team balanced innovation with accountability, scaled adoption from 500 to 25,000 developers, and measured success through sentiment and productivity. More than a deployment, this is a story of trust, enablement, and putting developers—our customers—at the center.
