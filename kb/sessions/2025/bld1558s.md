# Agentic AI essentials: Building an AI agent to triage GitHub Issues

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 11:50 AM–12:50 PM PDT** · [Sandbox](../../rooms/sandbox-firehouse.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Pooja Mistry](../../people/pooja-mistry.md) (facilitator), [Daniel](../../people/daniel.md) (facilitator)

**Hosted by:** [Postman](../../organizations/postman.md)

Agentic AI is here. In this interactive workshop, you will learn the essentials of building and customizing your own AI agent. We will design an agent that automatically summarizes and prioritizes GitHub Issues using sentiment, reactions, comment volume, and competitor mentions. You will also learn how to extend its capabilities, trigger it through Slack or other inputs, and integrate it into your workflow with a slash command. By the end, you will have a fully functional agent you can run locally and adapt to your team’s needs.

- **Type:** Sandbox Session
- **Track:** Build, Automate
- **Level:** Level 300: Advanced
- **Topics:** AI (Agentic AI, Generative AI, Fine-tuned modeling, Models)
- **GitHub products:** Actions, Projects and Issues
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developer
- **Mentions:** [GitHub Issues and Projects](../../software/github-issues-projects.yaml), [Slack](../../organizations/slack.md)
