# Transforming engineering ops with AI-powered deep research

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 3:45 PM–4:00 PM PDT** · [Demos & Donuts](../../rooms/demos-donuts-gateway-pavilion-level-2.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Jeff Keyes](../../people/jeff-keyes.md)

Engineering operations teams spend over 18 hours a week in "identify-analyze-execute" cycles—digging through Jira data, burndown charts, and endless interviews to piece together delivery progress. Without deep research applied to software development management, they produce static red/yellow/green PowerPoint summaries that often miss technical and delivery risks until deadlines hit. 

This session demonstrates how deep research methodology transforms engineering operations, showcasing automated risk analysis, workflow optimization, and requirements assessment.
