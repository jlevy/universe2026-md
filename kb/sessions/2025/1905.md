# Ship & Tell: Philips

**Wed Oct 29 2025, 12:50 PM–1:15 PM PDT** · [GitHub Central](../../rooms/github-central-festival-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [David Terol](../../people/david-terol.md)

Discover how Philips is transforming digital health innovation with innersource on GitHub, then meet the team behind the story. Learn how they brought together 6,000+ developers, made 85% of code shareable across teams, and reduced infrastructure costs by 80% with GitHub Actions. [Read the full Philips story here](https://github.com/customer-stories/philips), then join the team for an open Q&A where you can ask about scaling innersource, automating securely, and fostering collaboration across a global enterprise—no presentation, just real answers from the people who built it.

- **Type:** Ship & Tell
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership
- **Mentions:** [Philips](../../organizations/philips.md), [GitHub Actions](../../software/github-actions.yaml)
