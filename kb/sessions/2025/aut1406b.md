# Managing multiple organizations without multiplying the pain

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 2:30 PM–3:10 PM PDT** · [BATS Theatre](../../rooms/bats-theatre-fort-mason-building-b.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Greg Padak](../../people/greg-padak.md), [Patrick Knight](../../people/patrick-knight.md)

Enterprise teams and admins are under pressure to balance robust security with developer productivity — especially as organizations scale to thousands of repositories and multiple business units. In this session, we’ll share proven strategies for transforming access management from a bottleneck into a business accelerator. Learn how leading platform teams leverage GitHub’s enterprise governance features to standardize policies, empower developers with just the access they need, and dramatically reduce risk — without slowing down innovation. Whether you’re looking to streamline onboarding, automate compliance, or support cross-functional teams, discover how to unlock developer velocity and security at scale.

- **Type:** Breakout Session
- **Track:** Secure, Automate
- **Level:** Level 200: Intermediate
- **Topics:** DevOps and DevSecOps, Collaboration, Custom integrations and APIs, Governance and compliance
- **GitHub products:** GitHub Enterprise Cloud, GitHub Enterprise Server
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developer
