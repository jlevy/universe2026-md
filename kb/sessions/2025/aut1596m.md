# From metrics to impact: Turn GitHub Copilot data into business value

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 1:40 PM–2:10 PM PDT** · [Main Stage](../../rooms/main-stage-festival-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Sharanya Doddapaneni](../../people/sharanya-doddapaneni.md), [Shruti Corbett](../../people/shruti-corbett.md)

Your GitHub Copilot deployment is live — now what? This session explains how engineering leaders transform AI adoption from "nice to have" to "business critical" using GitHub’s Copilot Metrics to provide the foundation for strategic decision-making around your AI investments.

You'll discover the refreshed Copilot Metrics dashboard that delivers comprehensive usage insights you can act on today. Learn how to interpret these metrics to understand adoption patterns, identify high-performing teams, and recognize opportunities to increase Copilot utilization across your organization.

Learn real strategies for three essential business outcomes: measuring effective usage, driving broader adoption, and demonstrating tangible business value. You'll also explore practical approaches to help your teams maximize their Copilot experience and how to translate usage data into compelling ROI narratives that secure ongoing AI investment.

- **Type:** Main Stage
- **Track:** Automate
- **Level:** Level 200: Intermediate
- **Topics:** AI (Agentic AI, Generative AI, Fine-tuned modeling, Models), Data (analytics, management, privacy), Monitoring and observability, Productivity
- **GitHub products:** GitHub Copilot
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership
- **Mentions:** [GitHub Copilot](../../software/github-copilot.yaml)
