# Bitrise Connect San Francisco

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 6:00 PM–10:00 PM PDT** · [Flores San Francisco](../../rooms/flores-san-francisco-2030-union-st.yaml)

We’re celebrating 10 years of helping mobile developers achieve amazing things and would love for you to join us! Come together with the local mobile community for an evening of celebration, learning, great food and drinks, relaxed conversation, and, of course, cake.

*Insightful talks on mobile CI/CD, automation, and developer experience

*Connect with developers, engineers, and mobile leaders in the community

*Celebrate 10 years of Bitrise with a toast and cake

*All attendees receive 3 months of Bitrise Pro and a chance to win a Mac Mini M4 Pro
