# Immutable releases: How to create trust, transparency, and verifiability

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 11:30 AM–11:45 AM PDT** · [Silent Disco Demos](../../rooms/silent-disco-demos-festival-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Brian DeHamer](../../people/brian-dehamer.md)

Tag mutability has long been a major pain point for maintainers and consumers alike. But with Immutable Releases, GitHub is introducing a way for maintainers to enforce the immutability of releases and provide cryptographically verifiable attestations for their assets. This session will cover an introductory-to-intermediate explanation and demo of Immutable Releases and illustrate how they can enable greater trust, transparency, and verifiability for everyone consuming release assets.

- **Type:** Product Demo
- **Track:** Build, Secure, Automate
- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
- **Topics:** Open source, Security (RASP, supply chain, threat modeling, vulnerability detection)
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Developer, Open Source Developer or Maintainer, Security Professional
