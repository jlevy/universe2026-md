# Smarter by design: Spec-driven development with CoreStory and GitHub Copilot

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 11:30 AM–11:45 AM PDT** · [Demos & Donuts](../../rooms/demos-donuts-gateway-pavilion-level-2.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Anand Kulkarni](../../people/anand-kulkarni.md)

AI coding assistants like GitHub Copilot can accelerate development—but even the most capable AI assistant benefits from clear, structured specifications. In this 15-minute talk, CoreStory reveals how spec-driven development transforms Copilot into a predictable enterprise engine. See how specifications become executable intelligence that enforces 100% test coverage, auto-generates issues, triages bugs, and automates full epics—delivering threefold ROI and the equivalent of 20 to 50 virtual developers through context-aware automation.
