**Wed Oct 29 2025, 10:00 AM–10:45 AM PDT** · [Main Stage](../../rooms/main-stage-festival-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Martin Woodward](../../people/martin-woodward.md), [Bodhish Thomas](../../people/bodhish-thomas.md), [Gigin Chandy George](../../people/gigin-chandy-george.md), [Dylan Morley](../../people/dylan-morley.md), [Elliott Preston](../../people/elliott-preston.md), [Cindy Cui](../../people/cindy-cui.md), [Alison Co](../../people/alison-co.md), [Alethea Kramer](../../people/alethea-kramer.md), [Brian Park](../../people/brian-park.md), [Jayden Monney](../../people/jayden-monney.md), [Helen Hou-Sandi](../../people/helen-hou-sandi.md)

Calculators to code, sketches to shipped apps. The distance from idea to impact is collapsing. What once took months now takes days. But speed alone brings new questions: What gets built, and who decides? In this era of instant possibility, creativity, intent, and imagination matter more than ever — the work that truly moves the world forward.
