# Wind down the day and turn up the vibe with Eficode

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 6:30 PM–10:00 PM PDT** · [The Fog Harbor Fish House](../../rooms/the-fog-harbor-fish-house-39-pier.yaml)

After a full day of inspiration at GitHub Universe, swap session halls for salty air, hand-crafted cocktails, delicious bites, and sunset views with Eficode team.

We’ve reserved the private Eagle Cafe Transformed at Fog Harbor Fish House, a local legend that’s been praised as “the quintessential San Francisco waterfront dining experience.”

The Magic Hour Arrive just in time for the blue hour, when the sun dips and the city comes alive. Watch the Golden Gate glow in the distance, the Pier 39 Ferris wheel shimmer to life, and the skyline sparkle as the lights flicker on.

What to Expect:

Food & Drink: Delectable bites (no sit-down dinner, just plenty to graze) and refreshing drinks.

The Scene: Private space and deck, sweeping views, and a lively crowd.

RSVP required.
