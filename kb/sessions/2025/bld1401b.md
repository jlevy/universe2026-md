# From git to gate: How to customize Copilot to build trust in enterprise systems

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 3:30 PM–4:10 PM PDT** · [Cowell Theater](../../rooms/cowell-theater-gateway-pavilion-level-1.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Alzahrra Almajid](../../people/alzahrra-almajid.md), [Carl Charroux](../../people/carl-charroux.md)

Before Copilot can write a single line of code, you have to build trust. At United Airlines, that trust became the foundation for adoption across engineering teams. They started with small, low-risk tasks to build confidence — then customized Copilot using custom prompts, instructions, and agent mode to meet strict safety and compliance requirements. This session shares the strategies and lessons they used to make Copilot a trusted part of their workflow and how other enterprise teams can do the same in complex or regulated environments.
