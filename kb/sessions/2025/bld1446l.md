# Who are we now? Navigating developer roles in an AI-driven world

**Wed Oct 29 2025, 11:15 AM–12:15 PM PDT** · [Discussions Lounge](../../rooms/discussions-lounge-fort-mason-building-d.yaml)

**Speakers:** [April Gittens](../../people/april-gittens.md) (facilitator)

Join April Gittens, principal cloud advocate at Microsoft, and fellow developers in an interactive discussion about how AI is reshaping our professional identities. Come ready to share your own experiences with AI tools and explore how different developers are navigating role changes, skill evolution, and workflow adaptations.

Together, we'll discuss practical strategies for evolving alongside AI, share approaches for using AI-powered tools to accelerate learning, and explore how to maintain creativity and purpose in an AI-augmented world. Bring your questions, challenges, and successes — you'll learn from diverse developer experiences and leave with actionable insights from your peers.

- **Type:** Discussions Lounge
- **Track:** Build
- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
- **Topics:** Accessibility, AI (Agentic AI, Generative AI, Fine-tuned modeling, Models), Pair programming
- **GitHub products:** Codespaces, GitHub Copilot, VS Code
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Developer, Educators & Students
- **Mentions:** [Microsoft](../../organizations/microsoft.md)
