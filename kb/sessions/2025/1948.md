# Ship & Tell: Nordstrom

**Wed Oct 29 2025, 2:15 PM–2:40 PM PDT** · [GitHub Central](../../rooms/github-central-festival-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Anabelle Watt](../../people/anabelle-watt.md), [Ella Ordona](../../people/ella-ordona.md)

Heard Nordstrom's story about [turning their GitLab-to-GitHub migration into a complete CI/CD redesign?](https://reg.githubuniverse.com/flow/github/universe25/attendee-portal/page/sessioncatalog/session/1746202605226001qvYl) Now's your chance to go deeper with the team that built it. They just walked through their Standard Pipeline approach using reusable workflows, composite actions, and agent-assisted tooling that turned months of conversion work into weeks—join them for an open Q&A where you can ask about the technical decisions, the change management challenges, and the real-world tradeoffs. Whether you're planning your own migration, figuring out how to standardize CI/CD across teams, implementing AI-assisted tooling safely, or balancing speed with security guardrails—bring your questions and get answers directly from the engineers who lived it.

- **Type:** Ship & Tell
- **Topics:** Migration strategies
- **GitHub products:** GitHub Copilot
