# How to practice compassion (not criticism) during your code reviews

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 1:10 PM–2:10 PM PDT** · [Discussions Lounge](../../rooms/discussions-lounge-fort-mason-building-d.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Alex Oladele](../../people/alex-oladele.md) (facilitator)

Code reviews should be safe spaces for learning and collaboration. So why is the process often fraught with fear and anxiety? Join this interactive discussion where you'll share experiences, challenges, and solutions with fellow developers to create more compassionate code review practices.

Together, we'll explore practical strategies your teams can implement to reduce review stress and foster psychological safety. Come ready to discuss your current review processes, share what's worked (and what hasn't), and collaborate on solutions around automation, curiosity-based feedback language, and constructive disagreement techniques. You'll leave with actionable ideas informed by diverse team experiences and approaches.

- **Topics:** Git and code management techniques, Collaboration, Code quality, review, and completion
- **GitHub products:** Discussions, Projects and Issues
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developer, Open Source Developer or Maintainer
