# Co-work with code: Conversations on navigating leadership in the age of AI agents

**Wed Oct 29 2025, 11:00 AM–12:00 PM PDT** · [Recess!](../../rooms/recess-gateway-pavilion-level-2.yaml)

AI agents are here—and they’re part of your team. But what does it mean to lead them? In this lively session, join Accenture and Avenade as we dive into the real leadership shifts happening as humans and AI start working side by side. Expect laughs, insights, and honest conversation with peers as we explore how to build trust, foster collaboration, and lead with clarity in this new era. No prework required—just curiosity and a willingness to rethink what leadership looks like when your team includes both people and agents.

- **Type:** Social
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Security Leadership
- **Mentions:** [Accenture](../../organizations/accenture.md)
