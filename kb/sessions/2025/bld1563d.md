# Accelerate software development with agentic releases powered by JFrog

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 3:25 PM–3:40 PM PDT** · [Demos & Donuts](../../rooms/demos-donuts-gateway-pavilion-level-2.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Yonatan Arbel](../../people/yonatan-arbel.md)

**Hosted by:** [JFrog](../../organizations/jfrog.md)

Get to know JFrog Fly, the first Agentic Repository built for the AI era and tailor-made for the needs of small AI-native teams.
With Fly, you get zero-config software delivery. No setup, no scripts, just chat to build and deploy your packages and containers. It works OOTB with your AI-enabled IDE and Kubernetes. You get one place to store, share, and sync your artifacts from dev to prod and manage your software releases based on their semantic context.

- **Type:** Product Demo
- **Track:** Build, Automate
- **Level:** Level 100: Introductory
- **Topics:** AI (Agentic AI, Generative AI, Fine-tuned modeling, Models), Automated Infrastructure Deployment, Cloud-native development, DevOps and DevSecOps, Security (RASP, supply chain, threat modeling, vulnerability detection), Productivity, IDE Integration
- **GitHub products:** Actions, GitHub Copilot, VS Code
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Developer, Open Source Developer or Maintainer, Startups
- **Mentions:** [Kubernetes](../../software/kubernetes.yaml)
