# Ship & Tell: Cathay Pacific

**Wed Oct 29 2025, 4:00 PM–4:25 PM PDT** · [GitHub Central](../../rooms/github-central-festival-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Naveen Jaisankar](../../people/naveen-jaisankar.md), [Rajeev Nair](../../people/rajeev-nair.md)

Want to know how Cathay Pacific scaled GitHub Copilot and integrated AI tooling across 1,000 developers without falling into the Shadow AI trap? The team just shared their [playbook for moving from pilots to enterprise-wide adoption](https://reg.githubuniverse.com/flow/github/universe25/attendee-portal/page/sessioncatalog/session/1746068879225001Jws1)—now join them for an open Q&A where you can dig into the details. Whether you're dealing with alert fatigue, worried about LLM hallucinations, figuring out how to measure AI success, building governance frameworks that don't slow teams down, or simply trying to get developers to actually use the tools you've rolled out—bring your toughest questions about making AI a natural part of your SDLC, not an afterthought.

- **Type:** Ship & Tell
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developer
- **Mentions:** [Cathay Pacific](../../organizations/cathay-pacific.md), [GitHub Copilot](../../software/github-copilot.yaml)
