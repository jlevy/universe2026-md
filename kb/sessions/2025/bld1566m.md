# The AI shift: Frontline lessons in enterprise development

**Tue Oct 28 2025, 4:10 PM–4:40 PM PDT** · [Main Stage](../../rooms/main-stage-festival-pavilion.yaml)

**Speakers:** [Aaron Reich](../../people/aaron-reich.md), [Eric Bouguen](../../people/eric-bouguen.md), [Joseph Workman](../../people/joseph-workman.md)

**Hosted by:** [Accenture](../../organizations/accenture.md)

Discover how companies are harnessing GitHub, intelligent AI agents, and lessons learned from complex migrations to supercharge development velocity and transform delivery at scale. We’ll share real client stories and demos from the front lines, showcasing how organizations are modernizing tool chains, cutting cycle times, and unlocking new collaboration patterns with AI. Walk away with practical insights you can apply immediately to drive faster innovation and future-ready engineering practices.

- **Type:** Main Stage
- **Track:** Build, Automate
- **Level:** Level 200: Intermediate
- **Topics:** AI (Agentic AI, Generative AI, Fine-tuned modeling, Models), Cloud-native development, Collaboration, Code quality, review, and completion, Custom integrations and APIs
- **GitHub products:** GitHub Copilot
- **Audience:** Enterprise - Engineering Leadership, Enterprise - Developer, Open Source Developer or Maintainer
