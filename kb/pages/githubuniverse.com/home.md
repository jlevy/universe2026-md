- Access to streamed keynotes and content

## in-person/

Join in person to experience Universe and gain full access to sessions, partner showcases, and more.

sold-out!

- [Get notified](<https://reg.githubuniverse.com/flow/github/universe26/getnotified>)

## What's included

- Access to keynotes, sessions, panels, and demos

- Time to get your questions answered by GitHub experts

- Space to connect with peers

- A programmable badge

- Partner booths for interactive demos and practical solutions

- All day food and drinks. (And donuts, of course!)

- Access to exclusive merch at The GitHub Shop

## universe-on-the-road/

Universe is hitting the road this year! Join us in a city near you to learn what's new and what's next, connect with your local developer community, and experience what makes Universe so magical — closer to home.

free* *application required to attend

- [Find your city](<https://githubuniverse.com/on-the-road>)

## What's included

- Access to keynote, sessions, demos, and customer stories

- Time to get your questions answered by GitHub experts

- Space to connect with peers

- Partner booths for interactive demos and practical solutions

- Opportunities to deepen your technical skills
