# Explore

<https://reg.githubuniverse.com/flow/github/universe26/attendee-portal/page/explore>

## october 28–29 / fort mason center, san francisco, CA

explore-Universe/

From open source projects and pancake breakfasts to playful moments that bring attendees together.

[Get passes](https://githubuniverse.com/pricing)

## learn-and-build/

Build your skills and explore GitHub’s latest tools with help from the people who know them best.

## GitHub-central

Oct 28-29 | Festival Pavilion

Go beyond the main stage announcements and explore the latest from GitHub through live product journeys, interactive demos, a test kitchen with GitHub Next, and after party sessions with your favorite speakers.

![](<https://static.rainfocus.com/github/universe26/static/staticfile/staticfile/Session_Image_Github_central_1782426013041001tVGZ.jpg>)

## expert-center

Oct 28-29 | Gateway Pavilion

Got technical questions? Our specialized experts in AI, Actions, security, and scaled adoption are here to help you get the most out of GitHub — no matter your use case or team size.

![](<https://static.rainfocus.com/github/universe26/static/staticfile/staticfile/Session_Image_expert_center_1782426186299001jxE0.jpg>)

- [Book an appointment](<https://reg.githubuniverse.com/flow/github/universe26/expertcenter/>)

## connect-and-grow/

Chat with open source experts, connect with your peers, prep for your next role, and explore learning paths built to advance your skills and career.

## discussions-lounge

Oct 28-29 | Building D

Powered by Braindate, the Discussions Lounge is a space where you can curate conversations (or drive small group discussions) with like-minded people on the topics that spark your curiosity.

![](<https://static.rainfocus.com/github/universe26/static/staticfile/staticfile/explore-bd_1791470444412001DMJ1.jpg>)

- [Browse topics](<https://githubuniverse2026.braindate.com/>)

## level-up-lounge

Oct 28-29 | Gateway Pavilion

Pave the way for your next career move at Universe. Gain 1:1 advice from a career coach and build the practical skills you need through tutorials and certifications.

![](<https://static.rainfocus.com/github/universe26/static/staticfile/staticfile/Session_Image_Career_corner_1782426776084001oCJ4.png>)

- [Book an appointment](<https://reg.githubuniverse.com/flow/github/universe26/leveluplounge/page/home>)

## open-source-records

Oct 28-29 | Gateway Pavilion

Celebrate community-led software and connect with the people building it at Open Source Records, a record store concept where you can experience your favorite open source projects IRL.

![](<https://static.rainfocus.com/github/universe26/static/staticfile/staticfile/Session_Image_the_source_1782426841145001tCSK.jpg>)

## experiment-and-play/

Whether you’re swapping stories or making puzzles, the community spaces at Universe make connecting with your peers easy and fun.

Oct 28-29 | Gateway Pavilion

Every ticket includes a hackable badge — code it, customize it, and create a one-of-a-kind memento of your Universe experience.

![](<https://static.rainfocus.com/github/universe26/static/staticfile/staticfile/Session_Image_hack_your_badge_1782426928006001R1j2.jpg>)
